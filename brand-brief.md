# iGeeksBlog brand brief

Read this before making any motion video. Where it disagrees with RULES.md, RULES.md wins.

- **Name:** iGeeksBlog (handle @igeeksblog)
- **What it is:** Apple news, tips and reviews, plus AI, tech and gaming, written to help people get more out of their devices. Publishing since 2012.
- **Tagline:** "Your Gateway to Apple, Tech, and Gaming"
- **Website:** igeeksblog.com
- **Audience:** iPhone and Mac owners (largest market is India) who want the useful part fast: what changed, what to tap, and whether it matters to them.
- **Personality:** clear, useful, trustworthy, quick, curious
- **Logo:** the `Brand Logo/` folder

## 1. Brand in one line
We show you what changed on your Apple device and exactly what to do about it, using the real screen.

## 2. What viewers should feel after watching
"I get it, and I can do this now." They're smarter, not sold to. They trust us because every claim had proof on screen.

## 3. Colours (from the engine's palette, `src/components/Slide.tsx`)
| Colour | Hex | Where it's allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Black | #000000 | the background of every dark page (the default) |
| Card grey | #1B1B1E | cards and rows on black; nothing else |
| White | #FFFFFF | headlines and body text on dark |
| iOS blue | #4DA3FF (on light #0A6CFF) | the one accent: the "good" value, highlights, a live step |
| Pill yellow | #FFD60A | the single keyword pill, at most one per page, black text on it |
| Red | #FF453A (on light #E5322D) | a cost, a risk, the thing to switch off. Never decoration |
| Light ground | #F2F2F7 | light pages only, when the screen shown is light |

One accent per page. No gradients except the faint blue glow that drifts across the ground.

## 4. Font rules
- Inter only. Headlines 800, body 500-600. Left-aligned on one column.
- Never animate type on a continuous scale (it shakes). Type moves only while it arrives.
- Everything must read at phone size on mute. Captions are always on and never touch the content.

## 5. Logo rules
- Wordmark only: white (`igeeksblog-wordmark-white.png`) on dark, black (`-black.png`) on light. No other colours, no stretching, no effects.
- Use it on the end card and the cover only. Never as a corner bug or watermark.
- It must stand out from what's behind it (gate G69 checks this).

## 6. Voice and tone
- A reporter, not a hype account. Plain spoken English. Short sentences. Exact iOS wording for settings.
- Never say something more strongly than the evidence allows. Rumours are hedged. No manufactured outrage, no "you're being ripped off".
- Real product only. Any screen we redraw carries the label "Illustration · iOS 27 wording".
- Every post discloses the AI voice and presenter.

## 7. Banned looks
- A centred title on a gradient, everything fading in, corner labels, glow on UI chrome, generic particle bursts.
- A bare black page at a cut, an empty card waiting for its content, text that scales.
- A desktop screenshot fitted whole into a 9:16 frame, on-screen source credits (unless asked), more than one keyword pill per page.

## 8. How every video ends
An end card that the story earned. The ask is in XL type with one action word on the yellow pill. Under it, one line saying what the viewer gets, a supporting picture, and the presenter in the corner circle. The ask changes with the story: follow for part 2, save this, share it, answer a question, link in bio, or no ask at all. When a full article exists, the line is "Full story on iGeeksBlog (link in bio)". On long-form, leave the last 10 seconds clear for YouTube's end-screen tiles.
