# Nick Saraev style measurements

Use this as a compact directing reference. The engine's
`styles/nick-saraev.md` remains the detailed style source.

## Format

- 1080×1920 delivery, 30 fps.
- 26–48 seconds; default 32–42 seconds.
- Spoken pace: 202–252 WPM, weighted average about 233 WPM.
- Major visual change: average about every 1.8 seconds.
- Presenter: about 35–50% of runtime.
- Master: −14 LUFS integrated, ≤−1.2 dBTP.

## Script grammar

1. Immediate utility hook: free, new, top five, exact result, or surprising
   capability.
2. One-line definition.
3. Proof and mechanism, usually in numbered or sequential language.
4. Concrete examples, models, integrations, pricing, or setup steps.
5. Comment-gated CTA with a real companion resource.

## Visual grammar

- Opening: face plus the literal subject in a split composition.
- Cream, paper white, near-black, and coral/orange accent.
- SF Pro-style bold caption pills; 1–3 words.
- High-contrast italic editorial serif for display phrases.
- Pixel font and mascot only as accent.
- Rounded screenshot cards with shadow and a blurred/enlarged background echo.
- Real product UI, terminal, repository, model, chart, or benchmark evidence.
- Animated bars, counters, arrows, highlights, file-selection states, and
  dialog interactions.
- Mostly hard cuts, reframes, and short push-ins. Avoid transition-showreels.
- One visual idea per beat and one text system at a time.

## Quality killers

- Fake AI-generated product UI or unreadable text.
- Full-page screenshots shown without a focused crop.
- Flat cards with no internal movement.
- Long facecam holds.
- Text repeated simultaneously as display type and captions.
- Empty black space around a small asset.
- A hook visual that is merely related to, rather than literally depicting,
  the hook.
- Mixed palettes or unrelated visual styles in one reel.
