# Delivery QA

Do not deliver until every blocking item passes.

## Blocking

- Final file opens and is 1080×1920 at 30 fps.
- Voice, captions, and visual cuts remain synchronized through the final CTA.
- Every number, date, proper noun, command, and URL matches the approved script
  and a primary source.
- Hook frame names or visibly depicts the actual subject with sound off.
- Face is correctly framed and visible in the opening two seconds.
- No HeyGen lip-sync collapse is visible during a facecam beat.
- No caption or display text covers eyes, mouth, evidence, or UI labels.
- No repeated transcript text appears simultaneously as captions and display
  type.
- Receipts are readable, card-framed, and focused on the highlighted claim.
- No screenshot or UI text is cropped mid-word.
- Credits are visible for third-party footage and receipts.
- CTA giveaway exists and contains the promised resource.
- Integrated loudness is approximately −14 LUFS and true peak is ≤−1.2 dBTP.

## Visual critic

- Major visual change every 1.6–2.4 seconds on average.
- No visually static scene holds longer than about 2.5 seconds.
- No consecutive scene uses the same layout treatment.
- No flat empty region occupies more than about 30% of a frame.
- Every designed card has internal motion: fill, draw, count, stagger, scroll,
  cursor, highlight, or controlled camera movement.
- One palette and one art direction across the reel.
- Generated visuals do not impersonate factual receipts.

## Audio critic

- Voice is intelligible on phone speakers.
- Music remains roughly 18–22 LU below the voice.
- Nick-style small clicks/pops reinforce element landings without becoming a
  rhythm track.
- Ordinary cuts are silent.
- No digital clipping, abrupt music endings, repeated identical cue, or
  mispronounced product name.
