# Voice clone recording script

Read this out loud and record it. About 4 minutes. One file is fine — just
pause a couple of seconds between sections.

**The only instruction that matters: talk, don't read.** Imagine one person
across the table who is genuinely interested. The clone copies your RANGE,
not your words — so the lines below are built to make your voice move on
their own. Let them.

Two things to avoid, because they are what produced the current flat clone:
- Don't settle into one tone and stay there.
- Don't slow down to "sound professional." Your normal speed is right.

If you fluff a line, just say it again and keep rolling. Small imperfections
make a clone sound human.

---

## 1 · Cold opens — punchy, land hard on the key word

*Say each like it's the first line of a video and someone is about to scroll
past. Short gap between them. Hit the word in italics.*

Your Claude bill is mostly the *reading* — not the writing.

Apple just quietly killed the *charging brick*, and nobody noticed.

This costs *four dollars* a month and replaces software I paid *ninety* for.

Everyone's using this AI tool wrong. Including *me*, until last week.

Your iPhone has had this setting for *three years*. I found it yesterday.

Three tools cut my token bill in half. The *third* one surprised me.

---

## 2 · Explanation — calm, level, unhurried

*Drop your energy down. This is the register most of a video actually lives
in. Explaining, not selling.*

Here's how it actually works. Every time you send a message, the model
doesn't remember the last one — so the whole conversation gets sent again,
from the beginning, every single turn. That's why a short question near the
end of a long chat costs so much more than the same question at the start.
Nothing is broken. It's just how the thing is built.

And once you know that, the fix is obvious. You're not trying to write
shorter answers. You're trying to send a shorter conversation.

---

## 3 · The turn — this is the register the current clone is missing

*Genuine change of gear. Pitch up, slow slightly, then land. Like you're
about to tell someone the part they weren't expecting.*

But here's the thing nobody checks.

Now — this is where it gets strange.

And that's when I actually looked at the numbers.

Except it doesn't work like that. At all.

I assumed the same thing. I was wrong.

---

## 4 · Range drills — say these like you mean them

*This section exists purely to teach the clone your high and low. Commit to
each one, even if it feels theatrical in a quiet room. It won't sound
theatrical in the clone.*

**Real questions — let your voice rise at the end:**

So what's actually going on here?

Why would anyone build it that way?

You're telling me that was free the whole time?

**Genuine surprise — react, don't announce:**

Wait. Six hundred and sixty *million* tokens?

Oh, that's clever.

That is a *terrible* idea.

**Quiet — drop it right down, almost like an aside:**

I wouldn't say this on camera normally, but honestly, most of these tools
are the same product with a different logo.

Between you and me, the free tier is all anyone actually needs.

**Emphatic — full weight, slow down:**

Do not update yet. Wait.

This is the one that actually matters.

Nothing else on this list comes close.

---

## 5 · Closes — landing, slightly slower

*The last line of a video. Let it settle. Don't trail off — land it.*

The file on you didn't go away. You just got the key.

So before you buy the upgrade, check that one setting.

That's the whole trick. It took me a year to notice it.

Two minutes of setup. Saves you an hour every week.

And that's the part they don't put on the box.

---

## 6 · Loose talk — 30 seconds, unscripted

*Put the script down. Talk to the camera about anything you did today, the
way you'd tell a friend. Ramble a bit. This is the most valuable audio in the
whole recording, because it is the only part with no reading in it at all.*
