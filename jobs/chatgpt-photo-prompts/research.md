## NOT CLAIMED

- That OpenAI shipped a "photographer mode" or any named feature matching these prompts.
  It did not — Images 2.5 is a general editing/generation upgrade; the nine "modes" are one
  creator's prompt engineering on top of it, not an OpenAI product feature. The script never
  says "ChatGPT added" a mode, only that prompts using the upgrade are circulating.
- That the thread includes any before/after example images. It does not — a reply on the
  post itself ("A photography breakthrough announced with zero photos is a bold creative
  choice") flags this, and it is correct: checked by scrolling the full thread, no image
  attachments, text prompts only. The reel's visual proof of the CAPABILITY comes from
  OpenAI's own before/after examples on the announcement page, never staged as "made with
  Rachel Woods' exact prompt."
- The 50% latency cut and multi-platform availability from the OpenAI announcement. Both
  real and sourced (see SEARCHED below) but not spoken — the script's claim is about
  quality, not speed, so neither appears as a ledger CLAIM.
- That results are guaranteed or identical to a real photograph. OpenAI's own language is
  comparative ("more natural", "more likely to carry through"), not a guarantee, and the
  script does not oversell it as one ("reads like a real portrait session," not "is one").
- That any of the nine prompts is "the best" one. The reel picks three representative
  examples and offers the rest via the CTA; it ranks nothing.
