You've now got eight new Apple products landing before September's over. And two of them already shipped, two weeks before the September 9th keynote even starts.

The M6 Mac mini starts at $899. The Mac Studio with M5 Ultra starts at $5,499. Both ship September 22nd, so the wait for either one is nearly over.

The other six all ride on one keynote: September 9th, 10 AM Pacific.

First, iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. The Pro Max alone reportedly gets a bigger camera and battery, so the gap between the two models is going to matter more than just size.

Then Apple's first-ever foldable iPhone. 5.5 inches closed, 7.8 unfolded, so it closes like a phone and opens into a small tablet.

Meanwhile, Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 are expected too, possibly bringing back a ceramic case Apple hasn't sold since 2019.

And AirPods 5, standard and noise-cancelling, already spotted sitting inside Apple's own software before Apple said a word.

So don't get excited about all eight yet. That's two locked in and priced. Six are still leaks until September 9th proves them right.
