# Structure — apple-8-products-september

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`styles/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).
`script_approval.py propose` refuses while placeholder markers remain.

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer who assumes "Apple's September event" means one keynote on the 9th
discovers Apple already shipped two of its eight new products a full two
weeks before that keynote even opens — which matters because it splits the
month's hype into what is already locked in (priced, dated, real) and what
is still six separate rumors riding on one hour on stage.

## SHAPE (S17)

**News**, with a light **List** structure underneath it (the eight products
are the spine) — not a pure List, because a List shape would just queue the
eight in source order. The News frame supplies what a List can't: a single
organizing tension (confirmed vs. rumored) that reorders the eight into two
groups and gives the ending something to resolve.

## PROMISE (S2)

You'll know, in one watch, exactly which of Apple's eight incoming products
are real right now (priced, dated, official) and which are still just leaks
waiting on one keynote to become real.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: the hook's claim that two products already shipped while "the
biggest event hasn't even happened yet" — which implies the other six are a
different, less certain category, without saying so yet.
Paid off: the closing line names the count explicitly — "two are locked in,
six are still leaks — until September 9th tells us which ones are real" —
converting the hook's implication into the stated fact, then hands the
viewer a reason to come back (comment SEPTEMBER, confirmation promised when
the keynote lands).

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: Apple has eight products moving this month — two already announced,
six expected at the September 9 keynote.
WHY: Apple almost never ships hardware two weeks ahead of its own iPhone
keynote; splitting the announcement this way means the Mac refresh doesn't
compete with iPhone headlines for attention.
SO WHAT: the viewer should treat "eight new products" skeptically — six of
them are still rumor, not fact, until the keynote actually happens, so plan
(and spend) accordingly.

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Full spec sheets for M6 vs M5 Pro / M5 Max vs M5 Ultra (AI-performance
  multipliers, core counts, memory bandwidth) — a spec dump kills momentum;
  the price + ship date is what a viewer deciding whether to care needs.
- The Tim Cook -> John Ternus CEO transition — already the entire subject of
  `apple-surprise-and-shine` (approved 2026-08-27); repeating it here would
  be the same treatment twice, which STYLE-RULES.md's treatment-history rule
  exists to stop.
- The camera-equipped AirPods (B790/B798 projects) surfaced in the same
  macOS 26.7 leak — a 2026/2027 product, not part of this September's eight,
  and would blur the count the whole reel is built around.
- iPhone 18 Pro pricing rumors (up to $300 pricier) — unconfirmed, adds a
  second hedged number in the same breath as the confirmed Mac prices, risks
  the two categories bleeding together on a mute watch.
- The "which one are you waiting on, fold or Pro" debate `september-preview`
  already ran as its ending — this reel's loop is confirmed-vs-rumor, not a
  purchase debate, so the CTA is reframed around confirmation, not a pick.
