Every iPhone going back to 2019 gets iOS 27. Almost none of them get the part you've been reading about.
It lands next month with the new iPhones. Apple dropped zero devices — iPhone 11 and up, same list as iOS 26.
But supported now means three different phones.
Only three get the full Siri AI: iPhone 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, and iPhone Air.
The 16 line and 15 Pro get standard Apple Intelligence — the new Siri look, not the whole assistant.
And from the iPhone 15 down, no Apple Intelligence at all.
That line cuts through one model year. iPhone 15 Pro is in. iPhone 15 is out.
And the Siri behind that top tier is genuinely rebuilt — on models Apple co-developed with Google, using the technology behind Gemini. They run on-device and on Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Google never handles your data.
Unless you're in Europe. Siri AI won't ship in the EU at launch.
But the real story is underneath. Everyone gets the Liquid Glass slider — one continuous dial in Settings, Appearance, instead of iOS 26's two options.
And Apple says app launches are up to 30% faster. Check the footnote: they measured that on an iPhone 11 Pro Max.
That matters, because iOS 26 stalled. 74% of recent iPhones after 150 days, against 76% for iOS 18 — which only had 127 days.
But iOS 26 dropped three phones. iOS 27 drops none. Apple just removed the variable.
