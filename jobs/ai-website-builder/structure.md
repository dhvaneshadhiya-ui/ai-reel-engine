# Structure — ai-website-builder

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

Someone who has seen the "free AI builds your website in minutes" reels discovers
that Hugging Face's builder has moved into HuggingChat, where one button asks who
you are, looks you up, builds the page live and publishes it free, which matters
because they can have a personal site tonight, as long as they finish inside a
free allowance of ten cents a month.

## SHAPE (S17)

Discovery.

A single-tool walkthrough with a withheld catch. The reference reel sold
a 13-month-old version ("DeepSite v2, powered by DeepSeek, completely free"). Our
added value is the current truth: where it lives now, what it does step by step,
and the catch the hype reels skip. The hook is the strangest true thing about the
current version (it researches you before it builds), the middle is the flow in
the order the viewer meets it, the catch pays off the loop.

## PROMISE (S2)

A free AI that builds and publishes your personal website, how it works now, and
the one catch.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: sentence 3, "It's free to start, which is where the catch comes in."
Paid off: "The catch: a free account gets ten cents of AI credit a month."
Then the so-what ("So plan your changes and build it in one sitting.") turns the catch into advice, and
the CTA follows from it.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: HuggingChat's "Make my website" asks about you, researches you, builds a
one-page site live, edits it on request and deploys it as a free Hugging Face Space.
WHY: Hugging Face is folding DeepSite into HuggingChat; usage runs on monthly
inference credits, and free accounts get $0.10.
SO WHAT: it is a real way to get a personal site online for nothing, but the
allowance is small, so plan your requests and build it in one go.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook (~2-5s): the official HuggingChat promo (deepsite.hf.co/promo.mp4),
the DeepSite logo handing off to the HuggingChat logo, then "Prompt to Build" with
"Build me a personal portfolio website" being typed. The viewer sees the product
the hook named, doing the thing.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: What is this app called?  A: "It's called HuggingChat"
- Q: Wait, isn't this DeepSite?  A: "Hugging Face is moving its website builder, DeepSite, in here"
- Q: What do I have to give it?  A: "Tell it your name, what you do, your links and a style."
- Q: Can I fix things I don't like?  A: "Ask for a change and it edits only that part."
- Q: Where does the site live?  A: "puts it online as a free Hugging Face Space, with its own link"
- Q: Is it actually free?  A: "a free account gets ten cents of AI credit a month"

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- DeepSite v4 features (redesign by URL, multi-page, model picker, Kimi K2.5 default): the reel follows the new home; one product per 50s.
- The reference reel's claims "click to edit any part", "powered by DeepSeek", "v2": stale or not found in v4.
- Comparisons with Bolt, Lovable, v0, Cursor (free tiers researched): a different reel; would double the length.
- Version history, public/private toggle, "Made with HuggingChat" badge: true, cut for momentum; caption.
- Old DeepSite user complaints (edits deleting sections, login loops): those are DeepSite v4, not the HuggingChat builder.
- "Website in minutes": no measured time; we say "while you watch".
- PRO's $2 credit: one number too many after "nine dollars".
