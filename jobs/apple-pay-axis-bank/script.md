Apple Pay just went live in India, almost twelve years after it launched in the US. But right now, it only works with one bank's cards, and not at every shop.

That bank is Axis. Got an Axis credit card, Visa or Mastercard? Add it to Wallet from the Axis app or the plus button. The bank checks it once, by OTP or in its app.

From then on, you skip the OTP. Double-click the side button, Face ID, and hold it near the card machine.

That's new for India: card payments here need two checks, and for years the second was an SMS OTP. Since April, an RBI rule lets your phone's own face or fingerprint scan count.

Everyone else waits: Axis debit cards don't work, and neither do RuPay or UPI. HDFC, ICICI and SBI Card aren't in, and reports say they're still negotiating.

Now, those shops. Zomato, Blinkit and Croma are on Apple's list, but each counter's machine has to be switched on for it, so one might take it while the next won't. If yours fails, turn on Tap and Pay under usage limits in the Axis app.

Any iPhone XR or newer on iOS 18 works, and your rewards still count.
