## Launch / devices
- A: "Starting today, Apple Pay is available for users in India across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, with support for Mac coming soon."
- A fn1: "Requires iPhone XR or later with iOS 18 or later. Available on Apple Watch Series 6 or later with watchOS 11 or later."
- B: "Axis credit cards can currently be used with Apple Pay on Apple devices except Mac."
- D (VIA Axis): "first Indian bank to offer the service"; CONFLICT: "Over the next few weeks, customers will be able to make payments at merchants, within apps and online..."

## Cards
- A: "Customers with Axis Bank-issued Visa and Mastercard credit cards can add their cards to Apple Pay"
- A: "users will continue to earn the rewards and benefits offered by their cards."
- B: setup "an additional factor of verification like OTP or Bank App Verification may be required"
- No primary mention of debit, RuPay, UPI.

## Where / how
- A: "pay in stores, in apps, and on the web at retailers of all sizes — all without the need to type in PINs or use OTPs at checkout."
- A: "thousands of in-store and online merchants across India, as well as anywhere that accepts NFC transactions." vs later "accepted by millions of merchants across India" (internal conflict — avoid a count).
- A merchants: "Apple Store locations, Blinkit, Chaayos, Comet, Croma, Interflora, Ixigo, Le15 Patisserie, Reliance brands, Tata 1mg, Zomato, and more."
- A PSPs: "Cashfree, JusPay, Mswipe, Paytm, PayU, Pine Labs, Razorpay, Worldline".
- A: "Simply double-click the side button or Home button; authenticate using Face ID, Touch ID, or a device passcode if prompted; and hold the top of the iPhone ... near the contactless reader to pay."
- A setup: "add a card directly to Apple Wallet via the latest version of the Axis Bank app. Alternatively, simply open the Wallet app on iPhone, tap the plus sign"

## Security
- A: "the actual card numbers are not stored on the device or on Apple servers, nor are they shared by Apple with the merchant. Instead, a unique Device Account Number is assigned, encrypted, and securely stored in the Secure Element"
