# Structure — apple-pay-axis-bank

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

An Indian iPhone owner who has watched Apple Pay work everywhere else for
twelve years hears it is finally here, and discovers it works today with one
bank's credit cards and at counters whose machines are switched on for it,
which matters because it tells them whether they can tap tomorrow, how to set
it up, and what (debit, RuPay, UPI, the big three banks) still is not there.

## SHAPE (S17)

News, with a withheld limit. The headline everyone repeats is "Apple Pay is
in India"; the useful news is the fine print. Hook plants the limit (one
bank), then the how-to for those who have it, then WHY NOW (the OTP rule),
then the missing list for everyone else, then the counter caveat and fix.

## PROMISE (S2)

Whether Apple Pay works for you in India today, how to set it up, and what
still does not work.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: "But right now, it only works with one bank's cards, and not at every shop."
Paid off: "Now, those shops." returns to the counter caveat; the bank is named ("That bank is Axis"), and the second loop,
"what doesn't work yet", pays off for the non-Axis majority; the ending
returns to the counter ("Tell us below if the machine took it"), the one
question nobody can answer yet.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: Apple Pay is live in India, Axis Bank Visa/Mastercard credit cards only.
WHY: card payments here needed an SMS OTP as the second check; since April
2026 an RBI rule lets the phone's own biometrics count.
SO WHAT: Axis credit holders can tap today with Face ID (check Tap and Pay if
it fails); debit, RuPay, UPI and HDFC/ICICI/SBI Card users still wait.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: Apple's own launch image from the India newsroom
(iPhone and Apple Watch showing an Axis Bank card with "Done"), placed whole,
with the newsroom headline captured on mobile, so the viewer sees Apple's
document and an Axis card in Wallet before the bank is even named.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Does it work with my card?  A: "That bank is Axis."
- Q: Does my Axis debit card or RuPay card work? Can I use UPI?  A: "Axis debit cards don't work, and neither do RuPay or UPI."
- Q: What about HDFC, ICICI or SBI?  A: "HDFC, ICICI and SBI Card aren't in"
- Q: How do I set it up?  A: "Add it to Wallet from the Axis app or the plus button. The bank checks it once, by OTP or in its app."
- Q: Will it work at my shop?  A: "so one might take it while the next won't. If yours fails, turn on Tap and Pay under usage limits in the Axis app."
- Q: Does my old iPhone work? Do I lose my rewards?  A: "Any iPhone XR or newer on iOS 18 works, and your rewards still count."

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Merchant counts ("thousands" vs "millions" in the same Apple release).
- Apple's reported 0.2% fee ask (anonymous; a bank-side story).
- UPI volume figures: true, but the reel is about cards.
- Apple Watch / iPad / in-app / Mac: shown on screen, not narrated.
- Security (Device Account Number): cut for time.
- Samsung Pay / Google Pay card tap history: a different story.
- Co-branded Axis cards: no source either way.
