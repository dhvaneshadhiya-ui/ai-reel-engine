Apple just shipped iOS twenty seven point oh point one, the first update to iOS 27. It fixes three bugs. Two only hit one iPhone. The third could hit yours.

The first two belong to the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. When Face ID failed inside an app like Passwords, some of them froze and restarted. That was a software bug, so you don't need a new phone.

The second is in the camera: some 2x photos, shot at the widest aperture in certain light, came out with a color glitch. Apple says only a small number of phones had it.

Which leaves the one for everyone: pull down Notification Center and Control Center together, and your screen could stop responding to touch.

Should you update? Apple lists no security fixes, so there's no rush. On an 18 Pro, though, do it today. Everyone else, whenever it suits you. It's in Settings, General, Software Update.
