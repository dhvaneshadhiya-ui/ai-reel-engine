# Research plan — iphone-18-battery-prepare-to-ship

## Research question

Why does the iPhone 18 Pro Max need a "Prepare to Ship" battery mode that no
other iPhone has ever needed, what does it actually do to the phone, and what
would a viewer feel/see if they had to use it?

## Format

News (explainer shape likely — see structure.md once written).

## Subtopics (news reel required set)

1. **Primary source** — Apple's own support article (support.apple.com/en-us/127848):
   exact wording, menu path, timings, warnings, publish/update date if visible.
2. **Independent confirmation** — two non-Apple domains that did NOT copy each
   other (record VIA): MacRumors, 9to5Mac, others found. Already fetched both
   in initial scoping; subagent should verify no shared upstream and look for
   a third independent domain.
3. **Context** — what changed vs prior iPhones: exact/likely battery Wh figures
   for iPhone 18 Pro Max vs iPhone 17 Pro Max vs prior Pro Max models; the
   actual regulatory number (IATA/DOT/UN38.3 lithium-ion single-cell 20Wh /
   100Wh pack air-transport thresholds) — find the REGULATION itself, not just
   tech press paraphrase; why a bigger battery specifically crosses this line
   (single-cell vs multi-cell, e.g. why 16" MacBook Pro at 99.9Wh avoids it).
4. **Reaction** — analyst/user/forum reaction (MacRumors Forums thread already
   surfaced), any competitor or industry commentary, any repair-community
   (iFixit) reaction to a firmware-limited battery for shipping.
5. **Contradiction check** — actively search for anything disputing this: is
   this feature confirmed only via leak/beta or already live in shipping
   iOS 27 GA; any report that the 20Wh figure or the feature description is
   wrong, exaggerated, or clickbait; any other iPhone/device that has hit
   this limit before (precedent check).
6. **Official visuals** — Apple support page screenshots/diagrams of the
   Prepare to Ship UI (Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone >
   Prepare to Ship), the "Ready to Ship" red badge, and any Apple newsroom/
   marketing imagery of iPhone 18 Pro Max battery/size. Also check for
   screen-recording-able flow if we have a physical unit or must simulate.
