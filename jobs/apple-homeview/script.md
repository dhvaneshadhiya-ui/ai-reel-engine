Today Apple finally unveiled the smart display it reportedly held back for two years. It's called HomeView, it costs two hundred ninety-nine dollars, and it's missing something almost every Apple screen has.

HomeView is a seven-inch square display that sits on its own speaker or mounts flat on a wall, and both versions cost the same. It runs on the A18 chip, which powers Siri AI, Apple's rebuilt Siri, and that Siri is still in beta.

And it can tell your family apart. It recognizes up to six people by face, and each of them gets their own home screen.

From there you can lock the front door, watch up to nine camera feeds together, or take a FaceTime call while your hands are full.

What it can't do is add apps. You get Apple's own apps and some audio and video apps, but Bloomberg reports there's no App Store.

If that's fine with you, pre-orders open today in the US, UK, India, Canada and Australia, and it ships November thirteenth. Europe has to wait, since Siri AI isn't out in the EU yet.
