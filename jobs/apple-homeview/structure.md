# Structure — apple-homeview

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

An Apple user who has waited years for Apple's answer to the Echo Show
discovers it is finally here at $299 and knows each person in the house by
face, but it has no App Store and isn't sold in the EU, which matters because
those two limits decide whether it is worth pre-ordering today.

## SHAPE (S17)

Product announcement, with one withheld catch. The specs carry the middle in
Apple's own film; the catch (no App Store) is planted in the hook and paid off
before the buying details. Fits because it was announced today and the price,
date and limits are what a buyer needs.

## PROMISE (S2)

What Apple's new home screen does, what it costs, and the one thing it's missing.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: "it's missing something almost every Apple screen has."
Paid off: "What it can't do is add apps." … "there's no App Store" — then the ending turns it into
the comment question ("a screen in your kitchen that can't add apps").

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: $299 7-inch Siri AI display, tabletop or wall, A18, six faces.
WHY: it's the hub Apple reportedly held back for Siri; Siri AI is the point
of it, and still in beta.
SO WHAT: you can run your home from it and take calls hands-free, but you're
limited to Apple's apps; pre-order today, ships Nov 13, not in the EU.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: Apple's own film, "Introducing HomeView" with the
device on a counter (film 0:02-0:06), then the $299 price on the card.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: How much is it?  A: "it costs two hundred ninety-nine dollars"
- Q: When can I get it?  A: "pre-orders open today in the US, UK, India, Canada and Australia, and it ships November thirteenth"
- Q: Can I get it where I live?  A: "Europe has to wait, since Siri AI isn't out in the EU yet"
- Q: Can I install apps on it?  A: "Bloomberg reports there's no App Store"
- Q: What does it actually do?  A: "lock the front door, watch up to nine camera feeds together, or take a FaceTime call while your hands are full"

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Speaker detail (woofer, passive radiators, Spatial Audio): true, but a spec list.
- Colors (four tabletop, two wall): on screen, not spoken.
- Power Mount prices ($59.95-$129.95): accessory detail, a second story.
- Apple Home Collection with LG / Schneider: ships December, a separate story.
- Secure Exclaves privacy: strong but needs explaining; cut for pace.
- New Apple TV 4K and HomePod mini 2: same event, separate reels.
- Rumored $350 price vs actual $299: tempting contrast but rests on a leak.
- Echo Show comparison: no official comparison; rumor-era prices only.
- New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa: listed in release; trimmed for the ear.
