# Structure — carplay-youtube-free

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A CarPlay driver who assumes YouTube can't play on the car screen (there is
no CarPlay app for it) discovers one free App Store app mirrors the whole
iPhone to the display, with no adapter box, which matters because the long
charging stop or the kids in the back now have a big screen.

## SHAPE (S17)

Tutorial. One task, start to finish, in the order a hand does it. Result
shown first (no suspense about the outcome in a how-to), then the steps,
then the blocker said out loud (the Pro screen + time limit), then the
safety rule, then the CTA.

## PROMISE (S2)

YouTube on your CarPlay screen in a few taps, free, no extra hardware.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: "One screen on the way looks like a dead end. I'll get you past it." and the promise "one free app puts your whole iPhone screen on your car's display"
Paid off: "Open YouTube, play anything, and it plays on the big screen." (the dead-end screen planted in sentence three is paid off at "here's that screen: the Pro paywall. Close it.") — the
promised picture, shown working on our own car.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: CarTV mirrors the iPhone screen to CarPlay from a "Cast to Car" tab.
WHY: YouTube has no CarPlay app, and mirroring works with any app, even ones
without a cast button.
SO WHAT: no adapter box; free to try (time-limited, Pro removes it);
passengers or parked only.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook line: our own footage of YouTube playing on the car's
CarPlay display with the iPhone in hand showing the same video (user-filmed
shot A1).

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Is it actually free?  A: "The free version still works. It just has a time limit, which Pro removes"
- Q: Do I need to buy a box or adapter?  A: "with no adapter box"
- Q: Which app is it?  A: "Install the one called CarTV, M3U IPTV Player and Cast"
- Q: Does it work for other apps too?  A: "It works with any app, even ones without a cast button"
- Q: Can I watch while driving?  A: "This is for passengers, or for when you're parked. Never watch while driving"

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Exact free minutes ("40 free minutes" is one reviewer's word, not documented).
- Pro prices: they differ by storefront; the viewer sees the screen anyway.
- No Wi-Fi needed for mirroring: true and useful, but a sixth fact in a 55s how-to; moved to packaging.
- M3U/IPTV/Jellyfin features: the app's main pitch, irrelevant to this task. A second task is a second reel.
- Apple's iOS 26 parked-only AirPlay video: a different mechanism, would muddle.
- Audio/video delay on some cars (reviews): kept for packaging FAQ, not the reel.
