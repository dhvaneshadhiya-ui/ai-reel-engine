Validated with `python3 tools/packaging_check.py carplay-youtube-free`.

**Every field below is pasted VERBATIM into the place it names.**
There is no assembly step. Instagram has no HASHTAGS field: its
tags go at the END OF THE FIRST COMMENT, because that is where
they are posted. YouTube keeps one, because YouTube reads them
out of the description.

Instagram's hashtag maximum is **5** (past that it ignores all of
them, official since Aug 2025); YouTube's cap is 15 and the
recommended band is **3-5** on both.

## instagram

1. Install CarTV (M3U IPTV Player & Cast) from the App Store
2. Close the Pro screen. The free version works, with a time limit
3. Connect CarPlay and open CarTV on the car's display

## youtube

TITLE: Play YouTube on CarPlay for Free (No Adapter Box)
CAPTION: YouTube has no CarPlay app, but CarTV mirrors your whole iPhone screen to the car's display.

Get CarTV (App Store): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cartv-m3u-iptv-player-cast/id6800211040

1. Install CarTV (M3U IPTV Player & Cast)
2. Close the Pro screen. The free version works, with a time limit; Pro removes it
3. Connect your iPhone to CarPlay and open CarTV on the car's display
4. Tap the Cast to Car tab and start mirroring
5. Open YouTube and play anything

Mirroring needs no Wi-Fi. If CarTV is missing on the car screen: Settings > General > CarPlay > your car > Customize.

For passengers, or when parked. Never watch while driving.
