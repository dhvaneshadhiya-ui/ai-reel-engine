# Shot list — carplay-youtube-free (filmed by the user)

Car PARKED (in P / handbrake on) for every camera shot. Turn on Do Not Disturb first
so no notifications appear. Delete CarTV first if it is already installed, so the
intro and Pro screen appear. Drop every file into _sources/carplay-youtube-free/footage/.

## Screen recordings on the iPhone (Control Center > Screen Recording, portrait)
- B1  App Store > Search > type "CarTV" > results list (5s)
- B2  Tap Get on "CarTV - M3U IPTV Player & Cast", let it install (5s)
- B3  Open CarTV: swipe through the intro, the Pro screen appears, tap its close (X) (8-10s)
- B4  With CarPlay connected: tap the "Cast to Car" tab at the bottom, go through the
      start-mirroring prompt to the end (8-10s)
- B5  Open YouTube and play a video, ideally an iGeeksBlog video (6s)

## Camera shots (a second phone, VERTICAL, 1080p or 4K, 30fps, hold steady)
- A1  HERO: car screen playing YouTube, the iPhone held in front showing the same video (8s)
- A3  Car screen on the CarPlay home, a finger taps the CarTV icon (5s)
- A4  Over the shoulder: iPhone opens YouTube and taps a video, the car screen follows (10s)
- A5  A different app mirrored (Photos or Safari) on the car screen (4s)
- A6  Wide of the dashboard showing the car is parked, screen on (4s)

Note while filming: does the sound come out of the car speakers? (tells us whether
the script can say so)
