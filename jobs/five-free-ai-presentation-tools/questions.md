# Open questions — five-free-ai-presentation-tools

1. **The five picks.** Gamma, Claude, Pitch, PowerPoint Designer (web), Napkin. Canva was cut:
   its own pages disagree on whether a free account can generate a presentation, and a June
   2026 hands-on says it can't. Google Slides' Gemini and Copilot in PowerPoint are paid.
   Keep these five?

2. **CTA.** "Save this for the night before your next deck." A save fits a toolkit list better
   than a comment-for-DM. Or would you prefer a comment keyword (e.g. DECK) with the five links
   in DMs?

3. **Claude in the list.** It's the one general chatbot here, included because its free plan
   genuinely outputs a .pptx file (Anthropic's own help page). OK to include a Claude tool in
   an iGeeksBlog reel?

4. **Desktop visuals.** All five are desktop web apps, so the proof shots are the vendors' own
   desktop screenshots, cropped and zoomed on the relevant part (Rule 2 exception, reason in
   research/visuals.md).

5. **Treatment.** Presenter in the corner circle on the hook and end card only (~10s of HeyGen),
   one ElevenLabs voice track, music bed on, captions on.
