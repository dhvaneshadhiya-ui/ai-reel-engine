## PowerPoint
- Designer: "While you're putting content on a slide, Designer works in the background to match that content to professionally designed layouts." It "can turn text such as lists, processes, or timelines into an easily readable graphic." SRC https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/create-professional-slide-layouts-with-designer-53c77d7b-dc40-45c2-b684-81415eac0617
- "On PowerPoint for the web, design suggestions are available to everyone." / "On desktop versions of PowerPoint, only subscribers get design suggestions." (same page)
- Steps: Design tab > Design Suggestions > pick in right pane; first use may ask permission > Turn on.
- Platforms: Windows, Mac, web, iPad (not iPhone), Android tablets (not phones). "Designer goes online to get design ideas." No suggestions "when a slide has a shape or text box drawn on it".
- Copilot: "free plans and some Business plans don't come with Copilot access" SRC https://powerpoint.cloud.microsoft/create/en/copilot-in-powerpoint/
- Free web apps: "Get free access to Word, Excel, and PowerPoint using Microsoft 365 for the web" SRC https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/free-office-online-for-the-web
- Prices SRC https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing/individuals — Personal $9.99/mo or $99.99/yr.
- "60 AI credits a month" snippet is stale — DO NOT USE.
- Name clash: "Microsoft Designer ... 15 AI credits" reviews are the separate image app, not PowerPoint Designer.

## Google Slides
- All Slides AI support pages: "This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan." Also available via Workspace Experiments, "a trusted tester program for users on personal accounts"; "desktop and in English only". SRC https://support.google.com/docs/answer/17111393 , /16961475 , /16443280
- Feature table: Slides generate image / generate+edit slides under Google AI Pro and Ultra only. SRC https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13952129
- Launch 2026-07-23: https://workspace.google.com/blog/product-announcements/create-on-brand-presentations-in-minutes-with-gemini-in-google-slides
- Experiments: 18+, "quality and availability may vary", "some countries and languages", leaving is permanent. SRC https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447104
- Prices SRC https://gemini.google/us/subscriptions/?hl=en — Plus $4.99 (no Slides), Pro $19.99.
- VERDICT: not free for an ordinary account -> EXCLUDE from a "free" list.
