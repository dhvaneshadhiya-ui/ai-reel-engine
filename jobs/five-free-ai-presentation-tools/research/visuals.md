MOBILE-FIRST NOTE (Rule 2): all five products are desktop web editors. PowerPoint Designer
does not run on phones at all ("not iPhone … not phones"); Gamma, Pitch and Napkin publish
only desktop editor imagery, and a mobile capture of their marketing pages would show a
landing page, not the product working. So each proof is the vendor's own desktop screenshot,
CROPPED and MOVED inside a `screen` block (focus rects), never fitted whole.
