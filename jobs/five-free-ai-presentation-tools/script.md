Five AI tools that do the slow parts of a presentation, free, right now. Each free plan just stops somewhere different.

Gamma builds a whole designed deck from one prompt. Free is four hundred credits at signup that never top up, ten slides per prompt, and a Gamma badge on exports.

Got notes instead? Free Claude makes them into a real PowerPoint file. Turn on file creation in settings, and expect it to eat your limit faster.

Then Pitch fixes the deck by chat: tell it a slide is too wordy. Free is a hundred credits that never renew, and PowerPoint export costs extra.

Already in PowerPoint? On the web, Designer turns a bullet list into a timeline, free for everyone. Keep the file in OneDrive.

And for diagrams, Napkin draws a paragraph as one: five hundred credits a week, with its logo on every export.

So stack them: draft in Claude, import the file into Gamma, and spend credits only on the slides that matter.
