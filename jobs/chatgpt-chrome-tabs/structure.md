# Structure — chatgpt-chrome-tabs

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer who thinks of ChatGPT as a chat box discovers it can now click and
submit inside their own signed-in Chrome tabs, which matters because one
permission choice decides whether it ever asks first.

## SHAPE (S17)

**News, carried by one planted setting.** A real, official update (OpenAI's
own thread and docs, 15 Oct 2026), so News is the honest certainty. A plain
announcement would be a feature list; the setting that decides how much it
does without asking is the tension that turns it into a story with a payoff
and gives the viewer something to act on.

## PROMISE (S2)

You will see what ChatGPT can now do inside your Chrome tabs, watch OpenAI's
own demo of it submitting a form, learn how to switch it on, and learn the one
setting that decides whether it asks before it acts.

(check_script's detector reports NO OPEN LOOP because "one setting" is not a

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: in Work, ChatGPT drives your Chrome tabs: messages, forms, downloads,
tab groups. In Chat it only reads them.
WHY: it uses the sites you are already signed into, so it acts as you.
SO WHAT: the permission you pick (once / this site / all sites) is the line
between an assistant that checks with you and one that doesn't; OpenAI itself
flags "all sites" as elevated risk. And a Cancel bar lets you stop it.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: OpenAI's own demo footage (@ChatGPT, 15 Oct) of
ChatGPT typing into the Navan expense form ("Wellness benefit — yoga
classes"), on screen while "OpenAI shipped this on October 15th" is spoken.
The viewer sees ChatGPT typing in a real browser tab, the thing just claimed.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: What can it actually do in my tabs?  A: "sending messages, filling out forms, downloading files, even grouping your pile of tabs"
- Q: Will it act on accounts I'm logged into?  A: "that works on sites you're already signed into, like Gmail, LinkedIn and Salesforce"
- Q: How do I turn it on?  A: "open Settings, then Computer Use, and install the Chrome extension"
- Q: Does it do things without asking me?  A: "By default, ChatGPT asks before it touches each new website"
- Q: Can I stop it mid-task?  A: "Chrome shows a bar with a cancel button, so you can stop it any time"
- Q: Is it free?  A: NOT ANSWERED — OpenAI says the extension is on all plans, but whether Free gets Work tab control is disputed; answered hedged in the caption, not the voice.
