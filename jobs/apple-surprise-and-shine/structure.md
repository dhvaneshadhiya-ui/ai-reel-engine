# Structure — apple-surprise-and-shine

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`styles/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

One sentence, before anything else:
A viewer who expects "just another iPhone event" discovers this one is
different in a way that has nothing to do with the phone — it's the first
keynote of a new Apple CEO, and it's set to finally show the folding iPhone
everyone assumed was still a rumor — which matters because it's the biggest
change in who runs Apple's product story in 15 years, landing on the same
stage as Apple's biggest hardware gamble in a decade.

## SHAPE (S17)

News.

Chosen because there's exactly one hard fact today (Apple confirmed a date,
a time, and a tagline) sitting inside a much bigger context (leadership
change + a device category Apple has never shipped). The shape has to keep
those separated in the viewer's head at all times — confirmed vs expected —
or the reel becomes rumor dressed as announcement, which is the one thing
this reel cannot be about a leadership-and-launch story.

## PROMISE (S2)

By the end you'll know exactly what Apple actually confirmed today (not
leaked, not guessed — confirmed), why this particular event is different
from every iPhone launch before it, and what's still genuinely unknown two
weeks out.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: the hook opens on the tagline/invite ("Surprise and shine") and
withholds WHY this event is different from the fifteen before it — the
viewer is told "this one isn't like the others" before being told what
"the others" means.
Paid off: mid-script reveal that this is the first Ternus keynote (leadership
change), then the escalation that it may also be the first time Apple ships
a device it has never shipped before (the fold) — both loops close by the
final third, landing on "so two firsts, one event, two weeks away."

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: Apple sent invites for a September 9 event called "Surprise and shine."
WHY: this is the first product keynote under incoming CEO John Ternus, who
takes over from Tim Cook on September 1 — and the leading rumor is that
Apple will finally show its first folding iPhone.
SO WHAT: how this event goes — leadership debut plus a genuinely new product
category — sets the tone for the next few years of Apple hardware, not just
what's in this one keynote.

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Apple Watch Series 12 / Ultra 4, AirPods, iPad mini 8, HomePod mini specs —
  single-tier rumor detail with no bearing on the story engine (leadership +
  fold); would dilute momentum into a spec dump.
- Exact foldable pricing ranges ($1,999-$2,500+) — disputed/pre-crisis
  estimates across sources, not load-bearing to the "what's confirmed vs
  rumored" promise; mentioned only as "well over two grand" if used at all,
  hedged.
- iPad mini 8 / HomePod mini / Apple TV 4K — cut entirely, no story function.
- Detailed camera/chip spec breakdown (A20 Pro node, megapixels) — belongs to
  a future "what Apple announced" reel once the event actually happens, not
  this "save the date" story.
