# Daily idea scout

One file per morning, `YYYY-MM-DD.md`, written by the scheduled task
`daily-reel-ideas`. Each file is a shortlist of reel candidates that have
already passed `tools/idea_scout.py --check`: a real story engine, two
independent sources, a known format, and not a repeat of anything in `jobs/`.

**These are candidates, not commitments.** Pick one, run
`python3 scripts/new_job.py <slug>`, and the normal pipeline takes over —
research ledger, structure, script, your approval, voice, render.

**The SRC lines are a head start, not the research.** `research.md` still has
to be filled properly: every load-bearing claim with its own TIER, SRC and
the SPOKEN words that carry it.

**AUDIENCE lines are language, never evidence.** They tell you what people are
confused about and in whose words — useful for the angle and the hook. A forum
quote never becomes a claim.
