# Structure — youtube-subscriptions-tab

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer whose thumb has hit the Subscriptions button at the bottom of YouTube
for years learns it is gone from there, now "Subs" at the top of Home next to
the recommendations, which matters because that button was how many people
skipped the algorithm.

## SHAPE (S17)

**News:** the change (where it went), why it matters (the algorithm escape
hatch, from May's test complaints), what else lands with it (feeds, custom
feeds with real limits), who gets it, then a question back to the viewer.

## PROMISE (S2)

You will know where Subscriptions went, why some people mind, and what YouTube
added in its place.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: sentence 1, "some people are not happy about where it went", and "Why would a button move upset anyone?"
Paid off: "That bottom button was how a lot of them skipped the algorithm" and
the ending returns to the thumb: "once your thumb relearns it".

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: Subscriptions leaves the bottom bar; Subs sits in the Home top bar.
WHY: YouTube says it is shaping "a more seamless Home experience" with feeds.
SO WHAT: your subscriptions are now a reach away and one tab from the
recommendations; in return you get Live/Music feeds and prompt-built feeds.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: YouTube's own demo clip, the new top bar with "Subs"
second after Home, then the TeamYouTube post line "Subs (formerly
Subscriptions)".

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Is Subscriptions gone?  A: "It's renamed Subs, and it lives in the top bar on the Home tab, right after Home"
- Q: Why would anyone care?  A: "That bottom button was how a lot of them skipped the algorithm"
- Q: What did YouTube add?  A: "Live and Music get their own feeds"
- Q: What's the plus button / custom feeds?  A: "Type a topic or a mood, like guided meditations under ten minutes, and YouTube builds a feed around it"
- Q: Do I have it?  A: "Those custom feeds start in the US, in English. YouTube hasn't given a date for the new layout anywhere else"

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Navigation drawer removal: one outlet's observation.
- What replaces the bottom slot: not stated by YouTube for this rollout.
- Feeds syncing to desktop, prompt editing, 13+/search-history eligibility: detail.
- Exact 2017 date: "nearly a decade" carries it.
- The February "Most relevant" Subscriptions complaint: a different change.
