## instagram

CAPTION: YouTube is moving the Subscriptions button. After nearly a decade in the bottom bar of the Android and iPhone app, it's leaving.

It's renamed Subs, and it now sits in the top bar of the Home tab, right after Home.

When YouTube tested this in May, people pushed back. For a lot of them, that bottom button was the way to skip the algorithm and see only the channels they chose. Now it's a reach to the top of the screen, one tab over from the recommendations.

What else is in the top bar now:
1. Live and Music get their own feeds
2. Movies & TV shows up if you pay for something like a Primetime Channel
3. A new + button builds a custom feed from whatever you type, like "guided meditations under 10 minutes"

Free accounts can save 3 custom feeds. Premium members can save 8. Custom feeds are rolling out first in English, to signed-in viewers in the US. YouTube hasn't given a date for the new layout anywhere else.

Source: YouTube's Community post "New ways to explore YouTube from your Home page" (22 Oct 2026).
