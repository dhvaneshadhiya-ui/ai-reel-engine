## CLAIMS

- CLAIM: YouTube is moving Subscriptions out of the mobile app's bottom bar, where it sat for nearly a decade.
  TIER: multi
  SPOKEN: "For nearly a decade it sat in the bottom bar"
  SURPRISE: 70
  SRC: https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/474148940/new-ways-to-explore-youtube-from-your-home-page
  VIA: YouTube's own post ("Your Subs feed is moving to the top bar on the Home tab")
  SRC: https://9to5google.com/2026/10/22/youtube-subscriptions-android-ios/
  VIA: 9to5Google's own reporting; "nearly a decade (2017)", linking its 17 Apr 2017 report of the bottom-bar test that held Subscriptions

- CLAIM: On Android and iPhone, Subscriptions is renamed Subs and sits in the Home tab's top bar, second after Home.
  TIER: official
  SPOKEN: "on Android and iPhone, it's leaving. It's renamed Subs, and it lives in the top bar on the Home tab, right after Home"
  SURPRISE: 75
  SRC: https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/474148940/new-ways-to-explore-youtube-from-your-home-page
  VIA: YouTube ("Subs (formerly Subscriptions): Your Subs feed is moving to the top bar on the Home tab")
  SRC: https://x.com/UpdatesFromYT/status/2113362200412979580
  VIA: YouTube's official demo clip shows the order + Home Subs Movies & TV Music Live
  SRC: https://9to5google.com/2026/10/22/youtube-subscriptions-android-ios/
  VIA: 9to5Google ("Subs appears in the second position after Home"; Android and iOS)

- CLAIM: When YouTube tested the move in May, users complained the bottom button was how they skipped the algorithm, and that the top is harder to reach.
  TIER: single
  SPOKEN: "YouTube tested this in May, and people pushed back. That bottom button was how a lot of them skipped the algorithm"
  SURPRISE: 55
  SRC: https://9to5google.com/2026/05/18/youtube-removes-subscription-tab-new-test-mobile/
  VIA: 9to5Google's own hands-on of the May test (YouTube confirmed the experiment; author: harder to reach on tall phones)
  SRC: https://voice.lapaas.com/youtube-removes-dedicated-subscriptions-tab-from-mobile-app/
  VIA: Reddit threads on the May test (audience sentiment, not evidence: spoken attributed, as a complaint)

- CLAIM: Live and Music get dedicated feeds in the top bar; Movies & TV appears only for viewers with paid content such as Primetime Channels.
  TIER: official

- CLAIM: Custom feeds: tap +, describe what you want (YouTube's example: guided meditations under 10 minutes), get a feed. Up to 3 saved free, 8 with Premium.
  TIER: official
  SPOKEN: "Type a topic or a mood, like guided meditations under ten minutes, and YouTube builds a feed around it. Free accounts can save three. Premium members get eight"

  SRC: https://9to5google.com/2026/10/22/youtube-subscriptions-android-ios/

- CLAIM: Custom feeds roll out first in English to signed-in U.S. viewers (13+, search history on); no date given for the new top bar elsewhere.
