# Open questions — iphone-blocked-signs

**Beat plan (what you HEAR → what you SEE):**

| You hear | You see |
|---|---|
| "That 'Delivered' under your last text? It doesn't mean they haven't blocked you." | A blue iMessage bubble with "Delivered" under it; the label gets a question mark stamped on it |
| "Blocking on iPhone is built to be silent… you're never notified…" | Apple's own support page, captured on a phone, with its line "the contact won't get a notification" highlighted |
| "Most of the clues people check will mislead you. One still holds up, and it isn't in Messages." | The usual "signs" stacked as cards (Delivered, green bubble, ring count), two crossing out, one left glowing |
| "Since iOS 16.5, guides report it can show up even when you're blocked." | iPhone Life's guide on a phone, scrolling to its iOS 16.5 line |
| "A green bubble doesn't settle it either…" | A bubble flipping blue to green, with two labels: "iMessage off" and "moved to Android" |
| "Here's the clue that holds up: a phone call. If it rings once…" | A call screen counting rings: 1 ring, then voicemail; then 2+ rings marked "likely fine" |
| "…a phone that's off, a Focus, or Silence Unknown Callers…" | Real iOS 27 screens from the Simulator: the Focus page, then the Silence Unknown Callers setting |
| "And Apple says you can still leave a voicemail…" | Back to Apple's support page, its voicemail line highlighted |
| "So when every clue lines up… stop calling." | The clue cards lining up, then easing away |
| "Pass this to anyone still staring at 'Delivered'." | End card: the "Delivered" bubble again, with a share prompt |

1. **Presenter.** It's a how-to, so a face is optional. **I suggest an
   animated reel with your digital twin in a corner circle, only on the hook
   and the ending** (about 10 HeyGen credits, with ElevenLabs voicing the whole
   reel). The other options are a face-led reel or no face at all.

2. **What we can show.** You can't record the blocked side for real. It needs
   two phones and someone blocking you. So the bubble, the green flip and the
   ring count are built as animated screens. The Focus and Silence Unknown
   Callers screens are real iOS 27 from the Simulator, if it can show the
   Phone settings (I'll confirm when I scout). **If you'd like it authentic: block
   a spare number on your iPhone, call it from the other phone, and
   screen-record both (about 15 seconds).** That would replace the animated
   ring count. It's not required.

3. **What I cut on purpose:** the *67 hidden caller ID trick (it only works in
   the US, it fails against Silence Unknown Callers, and Macworld warns it can
   come across as harassment), FaceTime error messages (guides disagree on
   them), and the Focus "notifications silenced" sign (only one source, and it
   is contradicted). Tell me if you want any of them back.

4. **The CTA is a share** ("pass this to anyone still staring at
   Delivered"), because people send this kind of reel to a friend. The
   alternative is "save this." The ending also says "the kindest move is to
   stop calling." I think it's the right thing to tell people, and two
   sources raise harassment. Say if you'd rather end more neutrally.

5. **The iOS 16.5 "Delivered" claim** comes from several guides and an Apple
   Community thread, not from Apple itself, so it's said as "guides report."
