# Structure — iphone-blocked-signs

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer staring at a quiet chat, trusting the little "Delivered" label,
discovers that the label (and the green bubble) can't tell them anything,
and that only one clue — how the call rings — holds up, which matters because
Apple built blocking to send no signal at all, so knowing which clue to trust
is the whole answer.

## SHAPE (S17)

**Myth-busting, ending in a tutorial step.** Every guide online lists "signs",
and two of the most-checked ones (Delivered, green bubble) are unreliable. The
honest shape is: name the myth the viewer is relying on, knock it down, then
give the one test that does mean something, with its limits. A plain list of
ten signs would be the "facts in a queue" failure and would repeat the myths.

## PROMISE (S2)

You will learn which "blocked" signs on iPhone lie to you, and the one test
that actually points to a block.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: sentence 5 — "One still holds up, and it isn't in Messages."
Paid off: "Here's the clue that holds up: a phone call." The ending returns to
the hook's own word ("still staring at Delivered") per S18.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: Apple never notifies a blocked person; texts simply stop arriving.
WHY: so every "sign" is indirect, and Delivered / green bubble have innocent
causes (iOS 16.5 behaviour, iMessage off, Android).
SO WHAT: trust the ring count, allow for a Focus or a phone that's off, and if
everything lines up, take the answer and stop calling.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: Apple's own support page, captured on MOBILE, on screen
while "Apple's support page is blunt: you're never notified" is
spoken — the page's line "the contact won't get a notification that the call
or message was blocked" highlighted.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Does iPhone tell you when someone blocks you?  A: "Apple's support page is blunt: you're never notified"
- Q: If it says Delivered, am I not blocked?  A: "Since iOS 16.5, guides report it can show up even when you're blocked. So by itself, it proves nothing."
- Q: My messages turned green — am I blocked?  A: "It can just mean they turned off iMessage, or moved to Android."
- Q: What actually tells me?  A: "If it rings once, or not at all, and goes to voicemail every time, that points to a block."
- Q: Can I still leave a voicemail?  A: "Apple says you can still leave a voicemail. They just won't get a notification."

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- **The *67 / hidden caller ID trick.** US-only, defeated by Silence Unknown
  Callers, and two outlets warn it can read as harassment. Not something we
  should teach.
- **FaceTime errors.** Guides disagree on the exact error; network faults give
  the same result.
- **Focus status ("notifications silenced").** One outlet says seeing it rules
  out a block; a forum answer contradicts it. Too thin to speak.
- **How to block someone yourself** (Settings > Privacy & Security > Blocked
  Contacts). A second task; the other side of the story. Possible follow-up reel.
- **Blocked voicemails land in a separate folder.** True (Macworld) but it is
  the blocker's side and adds a beat.
- **"Check other apps."** Not an iPhone sign.
