## instagram

CAPTION: Seeing "Delivered" doesn't mean they haven't blocked you.

Apple never tells you when someone blocks you. Your texts just stop reaching them, so each sign below is only a clue.

"Delivered": since iOS 16.5, guides report it can show even when you're blocked. It proves nothing.
Green bubble: they may have turned off iMessage or moved to Android.
The call: one ring or none, then voicemail every time, points to a block. Two rings or more and you're likely fine.

A phone that's off, a Focus, or Silence Unknown Callers can sound exactly the same. And Apple says a blocked caller can still leave a voicemail. The other person just isn't notified.

If every clue lines up, give them space.

Source: Apple Support, "Block phone numbers, contacts, and emails on your iPhone or iPad".
