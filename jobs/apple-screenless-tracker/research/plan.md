# Research plan — apple-screenless-tracker

## Question
Is Apple really building a screenless, Whoop-style fitness tracker, what exactly
is it (form factor, sensors, positioning vs Apple Watch), who's behind it inside
Apple, and when (if ever) could it ship?

## Format
news

## Subtopics (required for news)

1. PRIMARY SOURCE — Bloomberg's exact reporting (Mark Gurman, Sept 22 2026):
   exact wording on form factor, sensors, executive backing, timeline. Date,
   byline, paywalled excerpt if available via syndication.
2. INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION — two non-Bloomberg-copying domains (9to5Mac,
   MacRumors, TechCrunch, The Verge, Athletech News, PYMNTS, Gadget Review) —
   record which ones add ANY original reporting/detail vs which just repeat
   Bloomberg (VIA: Bloomberg for all of them most likely).
3. CONTEXT — how this differs from Apple Watch (screen, always-on HR from the
   Aug 30 2026 leak), what Whoop/Oura actually do differently (no screen,
   subscription model, recovery/strain metrics), why now (Eddy Cue taking
   over Health post Jeff Williams Oct 2025).
4. REACTION — analyst/market reaction, Whoop's/Oura's public response if any,
   Oura's IPO context (3.6M rings sold), stock moves if any (Whoop is private).
5. WHAT WOULD CONTRADICT IT — search for Apple denials, "no such plans"
   statements, skepticism about accuracy of the report, history of Gurman/
   Bloomberg accuracy on Apple hardware leaks.
6. OFFICIAL VISUALS — there is no official Apple visual (this is an unannounced
   prototype). Identify what IS available: Whoop 5.0 / Oura Ring product shots
   (for comparison), Apple Watch official stills, Bloomberg article header
   image, any render/mockup articles have published (must be clearly labeled
   as unofficial mockups, not real product).
