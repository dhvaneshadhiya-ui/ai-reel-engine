# Independent Confirmation Check — Apple Whoop-style Screenless Fitness Tracker (Sept 22, 2026)

Research question: which outlets covering this story added original reporting/detail
vs. which just repeated Bloomberg's exclusive with no new information.

**Bottom line: ZERO true independent sources found.** Every outlet checked, including
The Verge, traces the story to a single origin: **Mark Gurman, Bloomberg**, published
2026-09-22 ("Apple Developing New Fitness Tracker Aimed at Rivaling Whoop",
https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/apple-is-developing-new-fitness-tracker-aimed-at-rivaling-whoop).
This is pure syndication with varying amounts of added context/framing, not
independent confirmation from separate sourcing.

## What I looked for and did NOT find

- **No outlet cited a second, independent source** (e.g., a supply-chain leaker, an
  FCC filing, a patent, a separate "sources familiar with the matter" chain distinct
  from Bloomberg's). Every hedge phrase ("reportedly," "is said to," "according to
  a report") points back to the same single Bloomberg story.
- **No named analyst or industry-expert quote specific to this device** in any of
  the five directly-fetched outlets (TechCrunch explicitly notes "Analyst Commentary:
  None present"). PYMNTS quotes Google's own marketing language about Fitbit Air, not
  independent analyst commentary on Apple's device.
- **No technical/spec detail beyond Bloomberg's own description** (thin fabric band,
  sensor-equipped computing module, no display, "technology investigation" phase,
  Tim Cook/Eddy Cue backing, not before 2028) — every outlet repeats this same set of
  facts, none adds a new spec, price, or timeline claim.
- **Could not directly access theverge.com** to confirm its exact wording/hedging —
  domain was blocked to the search tool and mirrors 403'd. Flagging this as an
  unresolved gap rather than asserting what The Verge's piece says.
- **No outlet disputed or contradicted Bloomberg's claim.**
