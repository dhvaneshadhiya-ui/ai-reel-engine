# Structure — chatgpt-plugins

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A ChatGPT user who thinks plugins died in 2024 (or never noticed them)
discovers they came back in July as a Plugin Directory, and that five of them
turn ChatGPT from something that answers into something that designs, edits,
plays, writes and protects, which matters because the last one can stop them
tapping a scam link today, for free.

## SHAPE (S17)

List with an escalating order and a planted payoff. Fun and visual first
(Canva, Adobe, Spotify), then the daily-use one (Gmail), then the one that
protects you (Malwarebytes) last, because the hook promised it. A flat list
of five equal tools is a listicle; holding the scam check to the end gives
the viewer a reason to stay through four picks.

## PROMISE (S2)

Five ChatGPT plugins worth installing, what each one does in one line, and
how to add one.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: hook, "the fifth one here can tell you if a text is a scam
before you tap the link."
Paid off: "And five, Malwarebytes. Paste the link, number or email you're not
sure about, and it rates the risk before you click or call." The ending
returns to it: "Then save this before the next strange text lands."

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: ChatGPT has plugins again: a directory of apps you install and call by
name.
WHY: OpenAI rebuilt its app store as a Plugin Directory in July; vendors like
Adobe folded their separate apps into one plugin.
SO WHAT: without leaving the chat you can make a design, edit a photo batch or
reformat a video, get music, clear your inbox, and check a suspicious link.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: Malwarebytes' official listing in OpenAI's Plugin
Directory (captured on mobile), its demo card showing a PayPal-invoice phone
number checked and flagged HIGH RISK. The viewer sees the scam check
happening before the list starts.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Wait, didn't ChatGPT plugins shut down?  A: "Since July, ChatGPT has plugins again"
- Q: Can it really spot a scam?  A: "Paste the link, number or email you're not sure about, and it rates the risk before you click or call."
- Q: Does it cost anything?  A: "It's free."
- Q: Do I need an Adobe account?  A: "even as a guest"
- Q: How do I get one?  A: "To add one, open Plugins in ChatGPT and tap Install."

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

  story; goes in the caption.
- Plugins now work in voice (2026-09-23): caption.

- Health (Apple Health + medical records): very relevant to iPhone owners, but
  health data needs more care than one line; a reel of its own.
- Instacart checkout, Apple Music, Uber, DoorDash, Tripadvisor: US-only or not
  in the directory as served to us.
- Google Drive/Calendar, Notion, Slack, GitHub: work tools; wrong audience for
  a five-pick consumer list.
- The @mention trigger: shown on screen in the listings, not spoken.
- Premium-only Spotify vibe playlists, Canva bulk create (Enterprise).
