## Canva
- Launch 2025-10-06 pilot partner. https://openai.com/index/introducing-apps-in-chatgpt/ : "Our pilot partners–Booking.com, Canva, Coursera, Figma, Expedia, Spotify and Zillow are also available today..."
- https://www.canva.com/help/connect-chatgpt-to-canva/ : "You can create, edit, and preview designs directly in ChatGPT through the Canva App." / "find your existing designs ... resize or copy a design, or import one from a URL. You can turn an image into an editable design too." / "available for all Canva accounts, including Canva Free" / "Some actions, such as using Brand Templates, need a paid plan." / "Only designs you select in ChatGPT are saved" / "ChatGPT sometimes generates links that look like Canva links but don't point to a real design."
- Steps (Canva help): Settings → @ Plugins → connect Canva; describe design "and say you want it in Canva"; "Select an option to open it in Canva and continue editing."
- Newsroom https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/deep-research-integration-mcp-server/ : "edit text across entire Canva designs just by chatting"; "translate your presentation into Spanish, Japanese, or any language"; "rolling out to ChatGPT users on Free, Plus, and Pro plans outside the EU".
- https://chatgpt.com/apps/canva/ : "Translate, resize, or rework designs without starting over"; prompts "Canva, design an Instagram post promoting a weekend sale." / "Canva, resize this social post to a stories format."
- iPhone: UNRESOLVED (help has Desktop tab only).
