Since July, ChatGPT has plugins again, and the fifth one here can tell you if a text is a scam before you tap the link.

OpenAI turned its app store into a Plugin Directory, and these five are worth installing.

One, Canva: paste a brief, and it becomes social posts, a flyer, or a deck you can keep editing.

Two, Adobe. It used to be just Photoshop, but now it edits a batch of photos and turns a sideways video into a Reel, even as a guest.

Three, Spotify: ask for music for a run, and the songs show up right in the chat.

Four, Gmail, which sums up a long thread and drafts the reply, so all you do is check it.

And five, Malwarebytes. Paste the link, number or email you're not sure about, and it rates the risk before you click or call. It's free.

To add one, open Plugins in ChatGPT and tap Install. Then save this before the next strange text lands.
