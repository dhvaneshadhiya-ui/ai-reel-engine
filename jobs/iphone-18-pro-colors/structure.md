# Structure — iphone-18-pro-colors

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`styles/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).
Filled in below.

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer who thinks the iPhone 18 Pro's color lineup is already settled
discovers a brand-new leak this week that contradicts the widely-reported
list — which matters because Apple's own reveal is only seven days out, and
even the people with supply-chain access can't agree yet.

## SHAPE (S17)

News.

Chosen because the material IS breaking news: a specific leaker post, dated
this week, that changes a previously reported story. A Discovery or List
shape would flatten the "this just changed, and here's why that's notable"
tension into a static color catalogue — which is also the treatment the
prior `iphone18-colors` reel already used (Pantone-code reveal, "plum not
red"). This reel's engine is the DISAGREEMENT between two leaks, not a
single leaker's palette.

## PROMISE (S2)

You'll get the freshest leaked color lineup for the iPhone 18 Pro — and find
out why it doesn't match the story you may have already seen, one week
before Apple settles it for real.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: early line naming that a leaker just handed out a NEW list that
contradicts the one everyone already reported — "which colors are actually
real?"
Paid off: closing line does NOT resolve which list is right (nobody can,
honestly — both are single-source) — it resolves the loop by pointing to the
one date that will: Apple's own September 9th event, seven days out. This is
an honest, non-manufactured payoff (framework S18) — the tension was real
uncertainty, so the payoff is the real answer: wait seven days.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: A Weibo leaker says the iPhone 18 Pro ships in dark cherry, sky blue,
and black, with silver dropped entirely.
WHY: That directly contradicts a separate, independently-sourced report
(CAD drawings) that still has silver alive alongside different secondary
colors — the two leaks only agree on one color.
SO WHAT: This close to a launch, when even people with supply-chain access
disagree, it's a sign the color lineup itself may still be genuinely
unsettled inside Apple — so any list you see before September 9th, including
this one, is a guess worth holding loosely.

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- The foldable "iPhone Ultra" color rumors (silver/white + dark blue) — a
  separate product story already covered in `jobs/iphone18-colors`; including
  it here would double the scope and dilute the single tension this reel is
  built on.
- The exact Pantone codes (6076, 427C, 426C, 2121) and the "red vs
  purple-toned plum" debate — already the entire subject of the prior
  `iphone18-colors` reel. Repeating it would be the same information in the
  same treatment; this reel names Dark Cherry once and moves to what's NEW
  (the conflict), not back into the color-science weeds.
- Pricing / cost-negotiation reporting (Jeff Pu's component-cost claims) —
  real, but not a color claim; off-topic for this reel's promise.
- The "colors are usually finalized last" framing device — true and
  sourced, but a second explanatory tangent on top of the disagreement beat
  would blunt momentum in a 60-80s reel.
