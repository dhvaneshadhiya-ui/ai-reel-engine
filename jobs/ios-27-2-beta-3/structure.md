# Structure — ios-27-2-beta-3

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

An iPhone owner who sees "iOS 27.2 beta 3" and expects new features
discovers the biggest visible change is a battery icon, plus three small
Settings changes, which matters because a polish-only beta, with Apple's
October promise for Siri's new languages, means the public 27.2 is likely
weeks away.

## SHAPE (S17)

News, with a short list body. Beta 3 has no headline feature, so a
"here are the features" list would be four equal bullets and would quietly
imply Health and Siri languages are new. The engine is the oddity in the
hook (the biggest change is an icon) and the question it raises (why does
that matter?), answered by the release timing at the end.

## PROMISE (S2)

What actually changed in beta 3, and what it tells you about when 27.2
reaches everyone.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: hook, "the biggest change you'll notice is a battery icon.
That's a clue to when you'll get the update."
Paid off: "So when do you get it? Apple promised those five Siri languages
in October... twenty seven point two probably follows soon after."

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: beta 3 updates the Settings battery icon, brings back Type to Siri on the
side button, renames the Apple Intelligence Report to Private Cloud Compute
Report, and adds a silenced icon on pinned chats.
WHY: 27.2's big features (Health, Siri languages) landed in beta 1; late
betas are where Apple finishes the details.
SO WHAT: Apple promised the Siri languages in October and Duo ships Oct 23,
so the public update is likely close; Type to Siri on the side button is
something a viewer can use the day it lands.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: the real Software Update screen reading "iOS 27.2
Beta 3" (iClarified screenshot), then the battery icon beta 2 vs beta 3
side by side from a tester's iPhone, so the viewer sees the build and the
icon before the explanation starts.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Is the new Health app in this beta?  A: "The big features of twenty seven point two already landed in beta one: the new Health app, and Siri AI in five more languages."
- Q: What's actually new in beta 3?  A: "Open Settings, Battery. The icon finally matches the new one in the corner of your screen."
- Q: Can I use Siri without talking?  A: "holding the side button can open Type to Siri again, so you type instead of talking"
- Q: When does everyone get 27.2?  A: "twenty seven point two probably follows soon after"
