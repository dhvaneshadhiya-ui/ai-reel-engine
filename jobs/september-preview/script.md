Apple is about to drop at least 5 new products this September.
First: iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. A20 Pro chip on 2 nanometers, a variable-aperture camera, a smaller Dynamic Island, and a new Dark Cherry color.
The catch? Rumors put them up to $300 pricier.
Second: the headliner — Apple's first foldable, the iPhone Ultra.
5.5 inches closed, 7.8 unfolded — basically an iPad mini that fits in your pocket.
Touch ID instead of Face ID, dual batteries, and a hinge built to hide the crease.
The price? Somewhere between $2,000 and $2,500.
Third: Watch Series 12 — faster chip, bigger battery, same look.
Fourth: Watch Ultra 4, possibly thinner with upgraded sensors — though that's a single source, so squint.
And fifth, the real wildcard: a new boss.
John Ternus runs his first keynote as CEO — Tim Cook officially becomes Executive Chairman on September 1. That part isn't a rumor.
What you won't see: the standard iPhone 18. That one moved to March.
The event lands September 8 or 9; preorders the same week.
So — the $2,000 foldable, or the Pro: which one's your money on?
