## Narration

Apple's iPhone 18 Pro is expected on September 9.
Three changes are real upgrades. Only one you'd notice on day one — and here's what makes it different.

Start with the camera. It's doing something phones have never done.
The main 48MP lens gets a mechanical iris. It physically opens and closes.
Wide in a dark room. Narrow when you want the whole frame sharp.

Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo says that lens alone costs Apple about 50% more than the one it replaces.
That's how much they're betting on it.

But moving parts and heavy computation both cost battery.
So the A20 Pro matters more than it sounds — Apple's first 2nm chip, projected about 15% faster on 30% less power.
Pair that with a Pro Max battery about 10% bigger.

And then there's the one you'd only meet on a bad day.
The C2 modem is Apple's own, and it reportedly adds 5G over satellite.
Signal with no tower in sight.

So this isn't really a camera story.
That's a phone built to keep working where phones normally give up — in the dark, on a dead battery, out of range.

None of it is official until Apple says so.

Dark room, dead battery, or no bars: which one would you actually notice first?

## Asset gaps — flagged, not papered over

The manifest's own exclusions say the mockup **is a back view only**: it does not
show the display, the Dynamic Island, the aperture mechanism or the camera. Two
beats therefore have no honest footage:

- **Beat 4 (the iris moving).** This is the reel's whole premise and we cannot
  show it. Either scout a real variable-aperture demo (any camera lens will do —
  the mechanism is generic and does not need to be Apple's), or build it as
  motion graphics and say on screen that it is a diagram. What we must not do is
  cut to a static back-view mockup while the narration describes a moving part —
  that is Rule 3 broken, and framework S14.
- **Beat 13 (satellite signal).** Same problem, lower stakes.

Everything else binds to a real asset or to a component we have.

## Not claimed

Per the manifest: no price, no aperture range (sources disagree — f/1.4-f/2.8
vs f/1.5-f/2.8, so the script says neither), the 15% / 30% figures are Jeff Pu's
projections and are attributed as such, the ~10% battery is Pro Max only, and
September 9 is "expected", never confirmed. No aggregator outlet is named in the
narration (user rule 2026-08-14) — attribution is on screen.
