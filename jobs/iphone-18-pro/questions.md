**2. The lead is the camera, not the chip or the date.**
I opened on the mechanical iris — "for the first time, an iPhone lens will
physically open and close" — because it is the only genuinely new *mechanism* in
the lineup, and RULES §3 says lead on the mechanism, since dates are the softest
claim and spec bumps are the most forgettable. The September 9 date is beat 3,
framed as expected. Push back if you want the date or the A20 Pro up front.

**3. Four sourced features are on the cutting-room floor.**
To stay in the 60–80s band at 166 words I cut: 5G via satellite, the N2 wireless
chip, the simplified Camera Control button, and the 6.3"/6.9" LTPO+ displays.
All are sourced and in the manifest. Swapping any one in means dropping something
currently in — most likely the battery beat or the modem beat.

**4. The hero image is a MOCKUP and the reel says so on screen.**
MacRumors' own filename is `Four-iPhone-18-Pro-Colors-Mock-Feature.jpg`. It is not
