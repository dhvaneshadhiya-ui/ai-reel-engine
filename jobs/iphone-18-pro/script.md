Apple's iPhone 18 Pro is expected on September 9.
And if the reports hold, it changes more than any Pro in years, including one that only matters when you have no signal.
Start with the camera: a mechanical iris on the main lens that physically opens and closes.
Wide open in the dark. Stopped down when you want everything sharp.
Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo says that lens alone costs about 50% more.
But you'd notice this one more: the Dynamic Island could shrink about 35%, as Face ID's illuminator moves under the display.
Inside, the A20 Pro would be Apple's first 2nm chip: reportedly 15% faster, on 30% less power.
Pair that with a Pro Max battery about 10% bigger.
Back to that signal.
Apple's own C2 modem reportedly adds 5G over satellite. Coverage with no tower in sight.
So this isn't just a camera upgrade.
The screen, the chip, the signal, even the frosted back it's wrapped in, reportedly in a new Dark Cherry.
None of it is official until Apple says so.
But which one would actually make you upgrade?
