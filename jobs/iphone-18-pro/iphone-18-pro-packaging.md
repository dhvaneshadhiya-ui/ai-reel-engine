CAPTION: The iPhone 18 Pro is expected on September 9, and the headline change is one no iPhone has had before: a camera lens that physically opens and closes.

Here is what the reporting actually says - and what it does not.

THE CAMERA
A mechanical iris on the main 48MP lens. Wide open, it pulls in more light in the dark. Stopped down, it keeps the whole frame sharp. Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo first reported this in December 2024 and says the lens unit alone costs Apple about 50% more than the seven-element lens in the iPhone 17 Pro. Reports disagree on the exact f-stop range, so we do not quote one.

THE DYNAMIC ISLAND
Reported to shrink about 35%, from 20.76mm to 13.49mm, because Face ID's flood illuminator moves under the display. Measurements from leaker Ice Universe, January 2026.

THE A20 PRO
Apple's first chip on a 2nm process. Analyst Jeff Pu projects roughly 15% faster than the A19 Pro on about 30% less power. These are projections for an unreleased chip, not benchmarks.

BATTERY
The iPhone 18 Pro Max is reported to gain about 10% over the iPhone 17 Pro Max. Note this is the Max only - the smaller iPhone 18 Pro is reported close to flat.

5G VIA SATELLITE
Apple's own C2 modem, the third generation after C1 and C1X, reportedly adds 5G over satellite - a connection with no Wi-Fi and no cell tower.

THE DESIGN
A redesigned rear Ceramic Shield for a seamless one-piece back, in new colours including Dark Cherry.

WHAT WE ARE NOT CLAIMING
Apple has announced none of this. There is no press release, no keynote and no confirmation for any feature above. September 9 is the expected date, not a confirmed one. There is no confirmed price.
