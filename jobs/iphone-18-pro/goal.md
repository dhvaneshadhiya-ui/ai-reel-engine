# Goal: SHARE

Emotion: **awe, with a thread of "wait, really?"**
Mechanic: **result first, then how — and the sender has to look early.**

Chosen because the material has one genuinely share-able fact: a phone lens with
moving parts. Nobody sends a friend a spec list; they send "phones don't do
this." SAVE would need a finite numbered system this story doesn't have, LEAD
needs a deliverable there isn't one of, and FOLLOW would mean making the channel
the subject when the camera is more interesting than we are.

## What the goal buys us

It decides what evidence is worth keeping, which is the whole reason this stage
comes before scouting (showrunner step 1). Applied to 17 verified facts:

**KEEP** — the three that serve one idea, that the phone keeps working where
phones normally give up:
  - mechanical iris (dark rooms)
  - A20 Pro at 2nm, projected 15% faster on 30% less power + Pro Max battery
    (dead battery)
  - C2 modem with reported 5G via satellite (no signal)

**CUT** — true, sourced, and not part of this story:
  - Dynamic Island shrinking ~35% — visible, but it is a cosmetic change and
    belongs to a different thesis
  - frosted one-piece back, Dark Cherry finish, simplified Camera Control,
    display sizes, LTPO+, N2 wireless chip, the foldable

Nine facts became three ideas. Framework S11 and S21: a video with 20 facts can
retain worse than one with 6 connected ideas, and the previous draft of this
script proved it — 58% of its sentences carried a measurement and 9% connected
to the sentence before.
