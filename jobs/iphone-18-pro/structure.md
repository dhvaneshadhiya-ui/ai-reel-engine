# Structure — iphone-18-pro

Written after the fact for this reel, as the worked example. On the next reel it
comes BEFORE the first sentence, which is the whole point: none of the three
below can be added later by editing lines.

## SHAPE

**Explainer**, not News — framework S17.

The obvious pick was News (what happened → why it matters → what's changing →
what next), and it is what the weak draft did: nine features in source order.
But nothing has happened. Apple has announced nothing; every fact is reported or
projected. There is no event to report, so News has no engine.

Explainer has one: *question → context → how it works → example → implication*.
The question is what any of these changes actually do for you, and that is
answerable with the material we have.

This is a NARRATIVE shape and it is independent of `format`, which stays `news`
— 60-80s, 2s hook, 6-9 SFX. See formats/README-structure.md.

## PROMISE (S2)

That the viewer will leave knowing **which of the three changes they would
actually feel**, rather than what the spec sheet says.

## LOOP (S10)

**Withheld:** that the most useful change has nothing to do with the camera —
the thing every headline leads with.

**Paid off at:** the C2 modem beat, ~55s in, and again by the CTA, which asks
the viewer to answer the same question the opening posed.

Not a loop, and worth naming because the first draft used it: *"three changes
are coming"*. An enumeration announces the agenda and withholds nothing. The
open-loop detector in `tools/check_script.py` was wrong about exactly this until
2026-08-19.

## ESCALATION (S6)

**feature → practical impact**, three times, tightening:

    a lens that moves          -> shoots in the dark
    a 2nm chip                 -> the above does not cost you the day
    a modem Apple owns         -> the phone works where there is no phone

Each beat is more consequential than the last, and the third is the one nobody
puts in a headline. That ordering is the escalation; it is not the order the
sources present them in.

## WHAT THIS COSTS

Dynamic Island, the frosted back, Dark Cherry, Camera Control, display sizes,
the foldable — all verified, all cut. They are true facts that serve a different
story. See `goal.md`.
