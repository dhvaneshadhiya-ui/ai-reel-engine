# Structure — 5-chatgpt-features

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer who thinks ChatGPT is a text box they type into discovers five things it
already does that they've never turned on, which matters because most of them take one
tap and quietly make every future answer more useful.

## SHAPE (S17)

**List**, with a spine — same shape as five-free-ai-tools. The spine here is "this is
already sitting in your account": every item answers where it lives in the app and who
gets it (free vs paid), so five separate settings accumulate into one idea (there's more
in here than the chat box) instead of five random tips.

## PROMISE (S2)

By the end you know exactly where five real, currently-live ChatGPT features live in the
menus, what each one does differently, and whether you already have access.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: the hook's number — 150 million people a week talk to ChatGPT out loud — and the
immediate turn, "if that's never been you." That plants the premise that most viewers are
missing things other people already use.
Paid off: the close ("save this, then go open Settings and turn them on") turns the
premise into an action — the loop closes by handing the viewer the one thing they can do
in the next minute to stop being the 150-million-minus-one.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: five ChatGPT features — Voice (Live), Tasks, Memory, data analysis, Projects — that
already exist in the product, most rebuilt or expanded in the last few months.
WHY: OpenAI has been shipping these as default-off or easy-to-miss additions layered onto
a chat box people already know how to use, so awareness lags capability.
SO WHAT: a viewer who turns even one of these on gets a materially different ChatGPT —
one that remembers them, acts without being reopened, reads their files, or keeps a whole
project's context together — for the cost of one settings visit.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook, the first stage scene is the Voice feature itself: a mobile capture
of the actual Voice icon / Settings > Voice > Live screen. It proves the hook's number
immediately — this is the literal surface 150 million people a week are using — before
the reel moves to the four things most viewers have not turned on.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Are these actually new, or old features repackaged? A: "Voice got rebuilt this July"
- Q: Do I need to pay for any of this? A: "Free gets three uploads a day"
- Q: Where exactly do I find these in the app? A: NOT ANSWERED in narration by design —
  each stage card shows the exact menu path on screen (Settings > Voice > Live; Settings
  > Notifications > Manage tasks; Settings > Personalization > Memory; upload a file
  directly in chat; New project in the sidebar), which is where a viewer actually needs
  it (readable, pausable), not read aloud as a settings path.
- Q: Is ChatGPT reading my email by default just by being on a paid plan? A: "can watch
  things like Gmail for you" is framed as something paid plans CAN do, set up per task,
  not a default-on background behavior — the line right before it says tasks are things
  you ask ChatGPT to run.
- Q: What happened to Canvas / can I still make a custom GPT? A: NOT ANSWERED — deliberately
  cut (see WHAT WAS CUT). Both are real, researched, and explained in research.md, but
  adding "here's what got removed" would turn a five-item positive list into a mixed list
  for a question most of the target audience (people who don't use advanced features yet)
  never asked.

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Custom Instructions — real, live, well-sourced, and arguably the single most classic
  "underused ChatGPT feature." Cut for visual variety: the reel already opens with two
  personalization-style items (Voice, Memory); a third settings-box item back to back
  would flatten the list. Logged in research.md for a future reel.
- Canvas — cut because it no longer exists under that name (retired May 28, 2026,
  replaced by writing blocks/code blocks). Demonstrating "Canvas" as current UI would be
  wrong for a Sept 2026 reel. Its successor was considered as a 6th item but cut for
  runtime — the reel already has one code-adjacent item (data analysis).
- Custom GPTs / GPT Store — cut because personal-account GPT creation is frozen ahead of
  a Dec 11, 2026 full retirement. Recommending viewers go build one would be stale advice
  within months.
- Personality (Base style and tone) — cut; real and live, but changes tone only, thinner
  payoff than the other five for the runtime it would cost.
- The "most users only use basic chat" adoption-statistic angle — no named, checkable
  survey was found, only SEO-listicle claims and one search-snippet-only OpenAI
  business-guide line. Cut rather than stated as a number the reel can't back up; the
  150-million voice stat carries the "you might be missing this" job instead, since it is
  fully sourced.

## SOURCES

OpenAI Help Center (help.openai.com) articles for Voice, Tasks, Memory, Data analysis,
and Projects — each fetched directly (via the Claude Browser tool, since WebFetch 403s on
help.openai.com) and dated within the last ~1-3 months as of this research. Plus two
openai.com primary announcements ("Introducing GPT-Live," July 8 2026; "Dreaming: Better
memory for a more helpful ChatGPT," June 4 2026) and the live chatgpt.com pricing
comparison table. Full list with verbatim quotes and VIA lines in research.md and
research/findings_*.md. Two independent domains (help.openai.com, openai.com, chatgpt.com
— all OpenAI-controlled, appropriate for claims about what OpenAI's own product does).
