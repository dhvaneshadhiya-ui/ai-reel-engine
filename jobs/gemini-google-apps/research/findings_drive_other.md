# Findings — Gemini in Drive / Slides / Meet

**Google Drive — Ask Gemini (cross-file Q&A):** "Use 'Ask Gemini' in Drive to group
related files, folders, or emails... as sources. Then, ask questions to help connect
the dots across your data." Access: drive.google.com or Drive app → top right,
tap/click **Ask Gemini**; type a question. Answers carry citations and can draw on
Drive/Gmail/Chat/Calendar. Group sources into a saved **Project** (shareable
knowledge base). Paid only: "To use Gemini in Drive, you must have an eligible Google
Workspace or Google AI plan." (support.google.com/drive/answer/16963068, /16686008,
/16684520). GA announcement: rolled out April 22–May 6, 2026 to Business
Standard/Plus, Enterprise Standard/Plus, Google AI Pro/Ultra, Google AI Pro for
Education (workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/04/ask-gemini-in-drive-now-generally-available.html).

**AI-powered search:** natural-language queries in the Drive search bar surface an
"AI Overview" with citations (e.g. "Show me the slide decks... that Deepti shared
with me"); scope toggled via "AI Overview search" checkboxes. Same paid-plan
requirement. (support.google.com/drive/answer/16685111)

**File summarization:** select a file/folder on drive.google.com, click
**Summarise** in the toolbar (Docs/Sheets/Slides/PDFs/videos/images supported); "See
more" for depth. Limitation: "You can use Gemini to analyse only one folder at a
time." Paid plan required. (support.google.com/drive/answer/16686465)

**Google Slides — "Help me visualize":** menu path **Insert > Help me visualize >
Image** (or **Infographic**), prompt-driven generation/editing via "Gemini 3 Pro
image," with style + aspect ratio (1:1/16:9/9:16) controls. Paid plan or Workspace
Experiments trusted-tester program required. (support.google.com/docs/answer/16443280)

**Google Meet — "Take notes for me":** click the icon after joining, then **Start
taking notes**; auto-generates a Google Doc, live "Summary so far," organizer recap
email. Pre-enable via Calendar event → Meet → Video call options → Meeting records →
"Take notes with Gemini." Supports 8 languages, 15min–8hr meetings. Paid plan
required. (support.google.com/meet/answer/14754931)

**Meet — Translated captions:** More options > Settings > Captions > set meeting
language > turn on Translated captions > pick target language. Restricted to
specific paid Workspace editions; article doesn't explicitly brand it "Gemini"
(uncertain branding, flagged). (support.google.com/meet/answer/10964115)
