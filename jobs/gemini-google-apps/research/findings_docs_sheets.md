# Gemini in Google Docs & Sheets — source notes (claims ledger input)

Sources fetched directly (primary, support.google.com / workspace.google.com). Secondary/search-snippet-only claims are marked as such.

## Google Docs — support.google.com/docs/answer/14206696 (official Help Center: "Collaborate with Gemini in Google Docs")

- **Help me write (new text):** generates a fresh draft from a prompt. Access: click where you want to insert text, then select "Help me write"; enter the description; click **Create** to preview, then insert. SRC: https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/14143478?hl=en-EN — quote: `"Click where you want to insert new text" then select "Help me write"`.
- **Help me write (rewrite/query selection):** select existing text, click "Help me write" to get options like "Summarize or Shorten," or type a free prompt asking Gemini to act on the selection. SRC: https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/14143478?hl=en-EN — quote: `Select text, then click "Help me write"` / `Choose options like "Summarize or Shorten," or Enter a prompt asking Gemini to write something based on the selection`.
- **Content refinement (Refine toolbar):** select a passage, click the **Refine** button on the floating toolbar; it can "rewrite specific sections," adjust tone, and substantiate arguments with facts/citations. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14206696?hl=en.
- **Automatic/on-demand summarization:** opening the "Ask Gemini" side panel on a text-heavy document surfaces an automatic summary; can also be requested. Access: click the "Ask Gemini" icon top right. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14206696?hl=en.
- **Document creation from a prompt:** in the bottom prompt field of the Ask Gemini panel, describe the doc you want and click Submit; Gemini drafts a full new document. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14206696?hl=en.
- **Image generation:** in the Ask Gemini side panel, enter a prompt such as "Create an image of a dog with glasses" to generate an inline or cover image from a single prompt; marketing page phrases this as "create inline images or cover images, all from a single prompt." SRC: https://workspace.google.com/products/docs/ai — quote: `"create inline images or cover images, all from a single prompt"`.
- **File/Drive/Gmail/web referencing ("Sources"):** the bottom bar of the Ask Gemini panel has a "Sources" icon to pull in Drive files, Gmail, or web content so Gemini's answer/draft uses that context. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14206696?hl=en.
- **Tone/detail-level adjustment:** the marketing page states Gemini can adjust "tone" and optimize "the level of detail in your document." SRC: https://workspace.google.com/products/docs/ai — quotes: `tone`, `the level of detail in your document`.
- **Drafts from Drive files:** "create first drafts based on files in your Google Drive." SRC: https://workspace.google.com/products/docs/ai.
- **Q&A on the document / Drive:** "Ask Gemini in Docs" to locate specific details or "get a document's top takeaways." SRC: https://workspace.google.com/products/docs/ai.
- **Eligibility (all Docs features above):** the official help page requires "an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan." SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14206696?hl=en — quote: `eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan`. The marketing FAQ adds: "Gemini is automatically included in Google Docs for Workspace plan subscribers." SRC: https://workspace.google.com/products/docs/ai.
- **Mobile limitation (VIA, not independently fetched):** a search-result snippet references a Google Workspace Updates blog post saying "Help me write," "Help me create," and image generation were not available on mobile at that time — not confirmed by direct fetch here. VIA: Google Workspace Updates blog (unfetched).

## Google Sheets — support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410 (official Help Center: "Collaborate with Gemini in Google Sheets")

- **Create tables from a prompt:** click **Ask Gemini** (top right), enter a prompt (e.g. "Create a table for a full day team event"), then click **Insert**. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Create formulas:** click "Ask Gemini," or start typing `=` in a cell and use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl+Alt+G (Windows) / Cmd+Ctrl+G (Mac); describe the formula in plain language (e.g. "Create a formula that divides goals by games"); click the target cell, then **Insert**. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Fix formula errors:** hover over a cell showing a formula error and click **Fix** — "Gemini reviews the formula and analyzes the issue." SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en — quote: `"Gemini reviews the formula and analyzes the issue"`.
- **Data analysis & insights:** ask Gemini questions about the sheet's data (e.g. "Identify trends in this table") to get answers, trend identification, and predictions. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Chart/graph generation:** prompt Gemini to build a chart with specified axes/data, e.g. "Create a chart with date on the x-axis." SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Spreadsheet summarization:** automatic overview in the side panel, or via keyboard shortcut Cmd+Ctrl+N (Mac) / Ctrl+Alt+N (Windows). SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Image generation in Sheets:** Ask Gemini → prompt like "Create an image of a dog with glasses." SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Conditional formatting:** e.g. "Highlight rows where sales are greater than 500" — "Highlight cells based on specific criteria." SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Dropdowns:** e.g. "Create a dropdown in column A with options 'High,' 'Medium,' 'Low'." SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Pivot tables:** e.g. "Create a pivot table that shows the sum of sales by region" — "Summarize data in a new sheet or the specified range." SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Filtering / sorting / find-and-replace / delete or insert rows-columns / fill range / freeze rows-columns / number formatting / format-as-table / clear filters:** all documented as prompt-driven "Help me organize"-style actions from the same Ask Gemini panel — e.g. "Automate the process of searching for a specific text or value...and replacing it" (find & replace), "Fill a specified range of cells with a single value or a repeating pattern" (fill range), "Lock a specific row or column in place to make it visible...even when you scroll" (freeze). SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Checkboxes:** "Insert a checkbox into a specific cell or range of cells," e.g. "Add a checkbox at the beginning of the table and name it 'WATCHED'." SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **Summarize Drive files / Gmail context, web search integration, Gems, optimization-problem solving:** all listed on the same page as things you can ask the Ask Gemini panel for while in Sheets. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.
- **File-format caveat:** features work on native Google Sheets files; an uploaded .xlsx must be converted first via **File > Save as Google Sheets**. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en, confirmed again on https://support.google.com/docs/answer/16959434?hl=en.
- **Eligibility:** "requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan" (same phrasing as Docs). SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14356410?hl=en.

## "Help me organize" / Build or edit entire spreadsheets — support.google.com/docs/answer/16959434 (official: "Build or edit entire spreadsheets with Gemini in Sheets")

- **What it does:** lets you "Create new sheets" and "Complete end-to-end tasks on existing sheets" from one prompt — e.g. generate an expense tracker or project template, or run an analysis across an existing sheet. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/16959434?hl=en — quotes: `"Create new sheets"`, `"Complete end-to-end tasks on existing sheets"`.
- **Exact access path:** open Google Sheets; on the right, "the side panel automatically opens with Build" — type your prompt there. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/16959434?hl=en — quote: `"On the right, the side panel automatically opens with Build."`
- **Workflow:** after submitting a prompt, "Review the plan and the template outline that Gemini provides" before it builds; a stop button cancels generation in progress; sources can be attached to guide the build. SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/16959434?hl=en — quote: `"Review the plan and the template outline that Gemini provides."`
- **Eligibility:** "requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan." SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/16959434?hl=en.

## AI function / `=AI()` formula — support.google.com/docs/answer/15877199 (official: "Use the AI function in Google Sheets")

- **What it does:** an in-cell function that can "Generate text," "Summarize information," "Categorize information," "Analyze sentiment," and "Access real-time information from Google Search." SRC: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/15877199?hl=en (quotes match exactly).
- **Syntax:** `AI("prompt", [optional range])` — the range is optional data from the sheet to feed the prompt. SRC: same page.
- **Access:** type `=AI()` or `=Gemini()` directly into a cell, or go to **Insert > Function > AI** from the menu; select the cell and click **Generate and Insert**. SRC: same page.
- **Limitation:** "The AI function doesn't have access to your entire spreadsheet or other files in your Google Drive" — relevant data must be pasted into the current sheet and referenced via the range argument. SRC: same page (exact quote).
- **Eligibility:** "requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan"; page framing suggests it may still be gated to a Workspace Experiments/Labs trusted-tester rollout (an "AI function not available" message can appear due to admin settings, plan, or language). SRC: same page.
