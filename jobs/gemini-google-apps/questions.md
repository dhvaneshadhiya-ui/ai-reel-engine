   (Rule 3).** Live-page checks turned up that Google's Help Center has NO screenshot
   at all for Docs' Refine toolbar or Sheets' formula/Fix flow (both are text-only
   instructions on their own pages) — so rather than narrate a feature with nothing

   Ask Gemini cross-source Q&A, **Meet** auto notes — all verified live and matched

   and image generation, Sheets formula generation and Fix, Drive's AI-search and
   folder summarization, Slides "Help me visualize" (narrower trusted-tester rollout).
   Happy to swap any of the five for one of these if you'd rather.

   CONFIRMATION BEAT (first visual is the real "Summarize this email" button, proving

   free). Docs -> Sheets -> Drive -> Meet follows roughly least-to-most novel, ending
   on Drive's fresh-this-spring GA and Meet's independently-flagged accuracy caveat.
