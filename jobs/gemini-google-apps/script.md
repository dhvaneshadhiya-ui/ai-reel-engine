Right now, five Gemini features are sitting inside apps you already have open, and most people have never touched the button.

In Gmail, open Gemini on any email and tap Summarize this email to shrink the whole thread into a short summary. That one's free, even on a personal account.

Docs does something similar: ask Gemini to summarize the page you're on, and it answers in bullets, with the sources it used.

Sheets goes further: ask Gemini to chart your data, and it pulls from every tab, not just the one you're on.

Drive can now pick sources across Drive, Gmail, and Chat, then answer questions using all of them. Google only finished rolling that out this spring.

And Meet takes it hands-off too: click one button, and Gemini takes the notes for the whole call, worth a skim since it could get a detail wrong.

Only Gmail's is free. Everything else here needs a paid Google AI or Workspace plan. Find the button, then decide if it's worth the plan.
