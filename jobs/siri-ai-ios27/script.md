You updated to iOS 27 and Siri still sounds like it did last year. That's because Siri AI stays off until you ask for it. Don't see that button? It comes down to three things, and I'll get to them.

Open Settings, scroll down and tap Siri. At the top, tap Try Siri AI, Beta. Then Continue, and Continue again on the privacy screen.
Depending on your iPhone, you'll then pick a voice and set its pace. Keep following the prompts.

After that, you might land on a waitlist. When your turn comes, the Siri app installs itself.

Now, those three things. First, you need an iPhone 15 Pro or newer, not just any phone that runs iOS 27. Second, your iPhone language and Siri language have to match, and for now that means English. Third, Siri AI isn't available in the EU or mainland China yet.

When it's ready, swipe down on the Dynamic Island and type, or just talk.
Apple also says it can take days to finish indexing your phone, so answers about your own stuff may be patchy at first.
