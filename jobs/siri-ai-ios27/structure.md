# Structure — siri-ai-ios27

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer who updated to iOS 27 and assumes the new Siri is already on discovers
it is opt-in, three taps deep and possibly behind a waitlist, which matters
because until they do it, their iPhone is still running the old Siri.

## SHAPE (S17)

Tutorial. One task (turn Siri AI on), steps in the order a hand performs them,
the blocker said out loud (the missing button, three causes), the finished
state shown (the Siri page with the BETA orb, then the Dynamic Island in use).
There is no reveal to withhold: the curiosity is "can I do this?".

## PROMISE (S2)

You'll turn on Siri AI in under a minute, and if the button isn't there, you'll
know which of three things is stopping you.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: sentence 3, "Don't see that button? It comes down to three things,
and I'll get to them." (count named, items withheld)
Paid off: "Now, those three things. First... Second... Third..." after the
steps, so the viewer who cannot find the button stays through the steps.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: iOS 27 ships Siri AI, but it is opt-in behind Settings > Siri > Try Siri AI (Beta).
WHY: Apple gates it by device, language match, region and a waitlist.
SO WHAT: updating is not enough; do the three taps now, check the three blockers
if it's missing, and expect personal answers to be patchy for the first days.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: Apple's own Settings > Siri screen with the blue
"Try Siri AI (Beta)" row (Apple Support video, 0:28-0:31 zoom), so the viewer
sees the exact button the reel is about before the first step is spoken.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Where is the setting?  A: "Open Settings, scroll down and tap Siri. At the top, tap Try Siri AI, Beta."
- Q: Why can't I see it on my iPhone?  A: "you need an iPhone 15 Pro or newer, not just any phone that runs iOS 27"
- Q: I tapped it, why isn't it working yet?  A: "you might land on a waitlist. When your turn comes, the Siri app installs itself."
- Q: Does it work in my language?  A: "your iPhone language and Siri language have to match, and for now that means English"
- Q: How do I actually use it once it's on?  A: "swipe down on the Dynamic Island and type, or just talk"

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Features (personal context, onscreen awareness, app actions): a different reel; this one is setup.
- Siri app tour (history grid, search, attachments): a follow-up reel.
- Gemini partnership: true and interesting, but it changes nothing about how you turn it on.
- Storage (up to 14 GB / 8 GB): not a step; would need its own explanation.
- Daily usage limits / iCloud+: no numbers published.
- Other languages arriving next: dates vary between Apple's "next month" and outlets' "iOS 27.1"; left out rather than hedged.
- Side button / "Hey Siri": viewers already know them.
