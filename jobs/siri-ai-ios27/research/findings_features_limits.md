## What (official N1)
beta, "rolling out today in beta in English"; "custom-built in collaboration with Google and its Gemini models"; "run on device and on servers using Private Cloud Compute". Do not say "Siri is Gemini".
## Features (N1 official)
personal context; onscreen awareness; app actions; web knowledge; follow-up conversations; dedicated Siri app (history syncs, MR); expressive voice (newest devices); Visual Intelligence; Write with Siri; own-style writing; Siri mode in Camera (A1); type or speak (A1).
## Requirements
- iPhones: "iPhone 16 models or later, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max" (N1, S1); S2 adds iPhone Air. official
- Voice customization + better dictation only 17 Pro / 17 Pro Max / Air (9A via S1, MR, EN) multi; S1 fetch didn't list — disputed
- Storage: "Up to 14 GB" 17 Pro/Pro Max/Air; "Up to 8 GB" other models (S1) official
- No iPhone RAM figure found anywhere.
- Language: "Available in English to start." (A1); French/Japanese/Korean/Portuguese/Spanish "next month" (N1); device + Siri language must match (S1). English variants disputed (MR 8 countries vs EN US only).
- EU: "will not be available initially in the EU on iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS" (N1); "no timeline" (N2); EU Mac/Vision Pro do get it.
- China: "will not be available in China while Apple works through regulatory requirements" (N1)
- Access: Settings > Siri > "Try Siri AI (Beta)", waitlist, "wait times can vary" (S2; 9B, MR, EN multi); under-13 excluded (N1); iOS 27.
- Cost: free, "subject to daily usage limits" server-side (N1); iCloud+ higher limits, no price (EN, TNW, MR). "required" claim disputed/wrong.
