## PRIMARY — Apple Support (every outlet VIAs this)
- Path: Settings > Siri > "Try Siri AI (Beta)"; if Siri off, turn it on first; choose voice, review privacy; "This will add you to a waitlist, and wait times can vary"; download starts automatically once admitted. SRC https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893 (dated 2026-09-22)
- "device language and Siri language are set to the same supported language" — English at launch. SRC same
- Not available to EU residents (iOS/iPadOS/watchOS) or mainland China Apple Account holders. SRC same
- Devices: iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 and later, iPhone Air; Apple Watch Series 9+/Ultra 2+/SE 3. SRC same
- Newsroom (search only, not fetched): https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/
