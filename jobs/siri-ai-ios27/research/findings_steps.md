## Official
- Apple Support 127893 (2026-09-22): "go to Settings, tap or click Siri, then tap or click Try Siri AI (Beta)." Row is "Siri", NOT "Apple Intelligence & Siri". https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893
- If Siri off: "tap or click Turn On Siri first." (same)
- Setup: "pick a voice, complete voice enrollment, and review your privacy settings" (same)
- Waitlist: "wait times can vary"; Siri AI "will begin to download automatically when your wait is complete." (same)
- Ways to invoke: side button hold, swipe down on Dynamic Island, "Hey Siri"/"Siri", Siri app — Newsroom https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/
