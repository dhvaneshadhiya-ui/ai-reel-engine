Apple made a demonstration video for a product it has never announced. Then it shipped that video inside a Mac update.

AirPods with cameras, running Visual Intelligence. Codename B790.

MacRumors' Aaron Perris found the file sitting in the macOS Tahoe 26.7 release candidate. Fully finished, Apple's own.

In it, a guy lifts a book toward his ear, and Siri answers: with Visual Intelligence, your world becomes savable.

It even warns you when your hair covers them.

So what's inside? Bloomberg's Mark Gurman says tiny infrared cameras — the same sensor Face ID uses.

Which means these don't take pictures. No photos, no recording. They just feed Siri whatever you're looking at.

When? Gurman says as soon as September, next to the iPhone 18 Pro. The name isn't locked: AirPods Pro 4, or AirPods Ultra. AirPods Pro 3 sit at $249, and this tier goes north of that.

One strange thing. In Apple's own video, you never once see the camera.

Earbuds that watch the room for you. Yes, or absolutely not?
