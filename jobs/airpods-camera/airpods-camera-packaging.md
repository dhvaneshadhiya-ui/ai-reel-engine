## instagram
CAPTION: Apple made a demonstration video for a product it has never announced — then shipped that video inside a Mac update.

MacRumors contributor Aaron Perris found the file sitting in the macOS Tahoe 26.7 release candidate: AirPods with cameras, codename B790, running Visual Intelligence.

In it, a guy lifts a book toward his ear and Siri answers: "with Visual Intelligence, your world becomes savable." There's even an alert for when your hair covers them.

Here's the part most coverage skips — these aren't cameras for taking pictures. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports tiny infrared sensors, the same kind behind Face ID. No photos, no recording. They just feed Siri whatever you're looking at.

Gurman says they could ship as soon as September, next to the iPhone 18 Pro. The name isn't locked: AirPods Pro 4, or AirPods Ultra. AirPods Pro 3 sit at $249, and this tier lands above that.

And one strange thing: in Apple's own video, you never once see the camera.
