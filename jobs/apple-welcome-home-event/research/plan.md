# Research plan — apple-welcome-home-event (news)

QUESTION: Apple has announced a "Welcome home" event for Tuesday Oct 13, 2026.
What exactly did Apple say, what is it widely expected to launch, how solid is
each rumor, and why does it matter to someone with (or without) an Apple home?

FORMAT: news

1. primary — Apple's own wording: Joswiak post, invite text, Apple Event page, date/time/place (findings_primary.md)
2. confirmation — two independent domains (MacRumors, Bloomberg/Gurman, 9to5Mac, Macworld), with VIA (findings_primary.md)
3. context — the rumored products: HomePad/home hub (J490, screen size, Siri/Apple Intelligence delay history, price), Apple TV 4K, HomePod mini 2, LG-made accessories; what changed vs past (Apple delayed the hub from 2025 waiting on Siri) (findings_products.md)
4. reaction — press/analyst/user reaction to the invite; tradition breaks (New York not Apple Park, second fall event, Oct 27 event rumor) (findings_reaction.md)
5. contradiction — anything saying the hub is NOT coming, rumors that conflict (screen size, name, price), what Apple has NOT confirmed (findings_reaction.md)
6. official visuals — Apple invite artwork, Apple Event page, existing Apple TV/HomePod mini product imagery, Apple Home app; simulator Home app (findings_visuals.md)
