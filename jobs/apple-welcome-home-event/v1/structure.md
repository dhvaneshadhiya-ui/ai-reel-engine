# Structure — apple-welcome-home-event

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

An Apple fan who sees a cryptic "Welcome home" invite discovers it is
the launch of a smart home hub Apple reportedly planned for 2024 and
held back because Siri wasn't ready, alongside the first Apple TV and
HomePod mini updates in years, which matters because anyone about to
buy either one should wait four days.

## SHAPE (S17)

News. The invite is the event; the tension is the two-year wait. A
product list alone would be three equal bullets; the Siri delay is what
EXPLAINS why three home devices land together, so it is the spine and
the list hangs off it.

## PROMISE (S2)

What "Welcome home" actually means: what Apple is expected to launch on
October 13, and why it took this long.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: hook, "Siri wasn't ready, so it waited."
Paid off: "All three arrive together for the same reason the hub was late. Gurman says each one is built around Siri AI"

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: Apple set an October 13 "Welcome home" launch in New York; Gurman
says a home hub, a new Apple TV 4K and a new HomePod mini.
WHY: all three are built around Siri AI, which is reportedly what
delayed them from 2024.
SO WHAT: Apple TV (2022) and HomePod mini (2020) are old; if you are
about to buy one, wait until Tuesday.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: Apple's own teaser (Greg Joswiak's post) — hands
stitching the rainbow logo, ending on the embroidery hoop reading
"Welcome home. October 13". The viewer sees the official invite before
any rumor is spoken.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: When is the event?  A: "October thirteenth, nine a.m. Eastern, in New York"
- Q: What is Apple launching?  A: "says three are coming"
- Q: What does the hub do?  A: "It runs your smart home, makes FaceTime calls, and can tell who's talking by voice or face"
- Q: Why did it take so long?  A: "Gurman says each one is built around Siri AI, the new Siri Apple showed in June"
- Q: Should I buy an Apple TV now?  A: "hold off until Tuesday"

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- October 27 Mac/iPad launch: a different event; would split the story.
- LG-built doorbell, lock, thermostat: "a few months" away, not Oct 13.
- J450 low-frame-rate camera: reportedly 2027.
- ~$350 hub price: one leaker (Kosutami), weak.
- Chip names (A17 Pro vs A19, S9, N1): conflicting reports.
- Wall-mount J491 model, larger robotic-arm hub (2027): detail.
- "HomePad" nickname: not Apple's and not Gurman's; would sound like a real name.
- Whether it streams: unconfirmed either way.
- Apple TV June price hike ($129 -> $199): true but a side road.
