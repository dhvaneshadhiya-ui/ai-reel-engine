# Structure — chatgpt-creator-prompts

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A creator who believes ChatGPT only writes generic slop discovers that the prompts
publishers keep recommending (and OpenAI's own) all start from something only the
creator has (their scripts, their audience, their transcript), which matters because
the generic version is exactly what audiences say they unfollow and what YouTube won't pay for.

## SHAPE (S17)

**List**, in workflow order, with one spine: every prompt names WHAT YOU PASTE IN
(scripts, audience, transcript, length/format, long transcript). The order follows a
creator's week (voice -> ideas -> hooks -> script -> repurpose), so the list builds rather
than queues: the voice set in #1 is what every later prompt writes in.

## PROMISE (S2)

Five copy-ready prompts for the actual jobs of a short-form creator, and the one thing
that makes them work.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: "Five prompts that do work, and every one starts with something only you have." — the viewer watches
each prompt for what "only you have" means this time.
Paid off: "Leave your material out and you're back to slop. YouTube won't pay for
mass-produced AI videos without your own insight." The ending returns to the hook's slop one level
up (S18): the input isn't a tip, it's the line between content and slop.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: five prompts (voice, ideas, hooks, script, repurpose).
WHY: a prompt with nothing of yours in it can only return the average — OpenAI's own prompts
leave a [paste details] slot for exactly this reason.
SO WHAT: set your voice once in a Project (free), then every prompt after it inherits it —
and your material is what keeps the output out of the slop audiences unfollow and YouTube
won't monetise.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: OpenAI Academy's own "ChatGPT for marketing" prompt pack, the
"Create a social post series" row, with "[paste details]" focused and marked — the
official prompt visibly asking for the user's own material.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Does this work on free ChatGPT?  A: "Free accounts get Projects too."
- Q: Why does ChatGPT's writing sound so generic?  A: "every one starts with something only you have"
- Q: Can't I just give it my YouTube link?  A: "paste a long video's transcript, not the link"
- Q: How do I make it sound like me?  A: "drop in your three best scripts, and tell it to write like those"
- Q: Does AI content actually hurt me?  A: "YouTube won't pay for mass-produced AI videos without your own insight."

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Caption and title prompts: they recur (5 and 3 publishers) but the reel already has five,
  and titles are the least specific to short-form video. Cut for momentum.
- Custom instructions: same idea as Projects; one feature per beat.
- Adobe's 86% of creators use AI / 85% want the final say: true, but a second statistic in
  the opening would bury the hook.
- The 56% "see slop often" figure: the Gen Z unfollow number is the one with a consequence.
- Free-plan upload cap (3 files a day): true, not needed for the steps shown.
- Any "this will go viral" framing: no publisher measures outcomes.
- Canvas: retired in May 2026.
