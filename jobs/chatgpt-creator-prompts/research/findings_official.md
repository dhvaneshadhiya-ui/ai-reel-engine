## A. Techniques
- Academy Prompting: "Outline the task" / "Give helpful context" / "Describe your ideal output"; "Outline what you want, who it's for, and why it matters."; tips "Ask for options", "Guide the tone and format", "Break big tasks into smaller steps".
- Enterprise prompting guide: role ("You are a customer support lead..."), audience, format/length ("100–200 words"), iterate ("Try something, fail, learn from the failure"), "ask me questions, then write up a clean prompt", "Treat your prompt like a handoff to a new intern".
- GPT-5 guide: "Poorly-constructed prompts containing contradictory or vague instructions can be more damaging to GPT-5 than to other models"; "extraordinarily receptive to prompt instructions surrounding verbosity, tone".
- API guide: few-shot — "including a handful of input/output examples in the prompt".
