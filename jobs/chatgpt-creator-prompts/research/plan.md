script, repurpose, packaging, audience feedback), what makes them work (per OpenAI's own
prompting guidance), and what ChatGPT features they rely on today (Sept 2026)?

FORMAT: ai-tools (list reel, animated treatment). Items are PROMPTS, not apps, so the product
subtopics are applied to ChatGPT itself plus the prompt set.

Previous reel on a neighbouring topic: chatgpt-photo-prompts (2026-09-17, image prompts).
