## Hooks
- D: "Write [number] hooks for this video: [paste transcript]. It's aimed at [audience] to get them [benefit]."
- S: "Suggest three short spoken opening hooks for this TikTok script. The goal is to capture attention in the first few seconds."
- R: "Write an intro that hooks the audience in the first 30 seconds by asking a provocative question"
## Scripts
- D: "Write a [tone] [platform] [video type] script that [benefit] in [number] [minutes/words]. Output in 2-column table with narration left, visuals right."
- H: "Act as a professional YouTube scriptwriter... Inject curiosity every 4-5 sentences..."
- Z1: "three scripts for a short-form video suitable for an Instagram Reel, TikTok, or YouTube short", "Each script should be about 100 words."; author pastes "a script from a recent video" to match voice.
## Repurposing
- S: "Identify three self-contained pull quotes from this webinar transcript that would work as standalone clips for a short social video."
- D: "[Paste transcript] Write a [Twitter thread/social carousel] based on key insights. Include final CTA."
- Ho: "Take this webpage and turn it into a 30-second TikTok script."
- H: "Repurpose this blog post into 10 LinkedIn posts..."
## Titles / captions
- D: "Write [number] headlines for [audience] providing [what] and [why]. Limit to [character count]."
- R: "Generate 5 compelling title options"; Ho: "Generate five YouTube video title ideas for a video about [TOPIC]"
- B: "Take this YouTube description and create a TikTok caption for the video"
## Audience / comments
- D: "What would [audience] like to know about [topic]?"
- S: "Review these comments ... assess whether they show early signs of a potential crisis."

## Why prompts work
- Name the audience (7/9). D: "A hook on the wrong audience is attention bait."
- Context/specific. S: "The more guidance you give upfront in a prompt, the stronger and more reliable the output will be."
- Paste your own past work: Z2 "samples of your past LinkedIn (or other social media) posts"; Z1 "a script from a recent video"; V "A title suggestion without knowing your niche, audience age, posting cadence, and the videos that already worked is just brainstorming." (2 publishers)
- Several options (R); iterate (Ho "Can you make it more casual?"; D "give it information and iterate").
- Critique prompt: no source.
