# Structure — meta-one-subscription

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`styles/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer who thinks of Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp as "the free apps"
discovers Meta just built a paywall ladder running from $2.99 to $499 a month
on top of them — which matters because the app that most loudly promised it
would never charge you (WhatsApp) is the cheapest rung on that ladder, one
year after it started running the ads it also once swore off.

## SHAPE (S17)

News / Product announcement, with a myth-busting turn in the middle.

WHY: this is a straight news event (a company launched a paid tier) — but
reporting only "here's what it costs" is a spec dump. The myth-busting turn
(WhatsApp's own founding promise, already half-broken by 2025's ads) is what
turns a price list into a story the viewer weren't already expecting from a
launch article.

## PROMISE (S2)

You'll know exactly what Meta is now charging for, on which app, and why
the cheapest tier is the one that should surprise you most.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: opening line questions whether "free" ever really meant free —
"The apps that promised they'd always be free just published a price list."
Paid off: closing line returns to it after the WhatsApp reveal — free was
never a permanent promise, it was a phase, and Meta just named the price
for what comes after it.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: Meta launched Meta One — tiered subscriptions from $2.99 (WhatsApp
Plus) up to $499/month (Max, for businesses), unlocking extra AI generation,
creator tools and app features across Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and
Meta AI.
WHY: Meta has poured billions into AI compute (the $14.3B Scale AI deal,
hiring Alexandr Wang) and is now asking the heaviest users of that compute
to help pay for it, while keeping the core free tier intact to avoid a
backlash.
SO WHAT: the free version isn't disappearing today — but WhatsApp's own
2025 pivot to ads, arriving one year before this subscription, shows what
"we'll always be free" is worth once a platform needs new revenue. The
ladder is a preview of where the pressure goes next.

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Exact per-tier feature lists (profile customization, super reactions,
  story replay analytics, Edits assistant) — too granular for 75s; the
  video keeps to the two facts that carry the story: what it costs, and
  what stays free.
- Appfigures' revenue jump numbers (475% IG daily revenue w/w) and the
  BNP Paribas / Truist long-range forecasts — interesting to an investor,
  not to the viewer this reel is for; cut for momentum, not because they're
  wrong.
- The March 2026 precursor launch (Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp Plus without
  the AI bundles) — folded into "already broken by ads" beat instead of a
  separate timeline point, to avoid a second history lesson competing with
  the WhatsApp one.
