# Structure — chatgpt-chrome-reads-tabs

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer who reads "OpenAI is bringing computer use to Chrome" and thinks
"ChatGPT already did this" learns they are half right: the side panel read
tabs since July, but acting inside tabs and the full app in the panel are new
on October 15.

## SHAPE (S17)

**News, following OpenAI's own thread (user's reference, 2026-10-19):** the
thread's two upgrades in its order (tabs: Chat reads / Work acts; then the
side panel gets the full app), the "isn't this old?" question its replies are
full of as the open loop, then the ask-first default, setup and plans.

## PROMISE (S2)

You will learn the two upgrades in the update, which part is actually new, and
how to turn it on.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: sentence 2 — "If that sounds like something ChatGPT already did,
you're half right."
Paid off: "Now the half you already knew. Since July, that side panel could
read your tabs. Acting inside them, and the full app in the panel, are what's
new." (S18; the detector misses "half" as a 4-letter word.)

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: ChatGPT uses your open tabs (Chat reads, Work acts) and the side panel
becomes the full ChatGPT.
WHY: OpenAI's framing: computer use for anyone on Chrome.
SO WHAT: comparing tabs is one question; chores inside tabs can be handed
off; it checks before acting on a new site; setup is the extension + @Chrome.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: OpenAI's own title card from its 15 Oct video ("Bring
your Chrome tabs into ChatGPT"), then the Chrome Web Store listing.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Didn't ChatGPT already do this?  A: "Since July, that side panel could read your tabs. Acting inside them, and the full app in the panel, are what's new."
- Q: What does it do with my tabs?  A: "In Chat, it looks across every one"
- Q: Can it do things for me?  A: "it can send messages, fill out forms, download files, and even group the pile you have open"
- Q: Will it act without asking?  A: "By default, it checks with you before it acts on a new website"
- Q: How do I get it, and is it paid?  A: "add the ChatGPT extension to Chrome and mention @Chrome. OpenAI says it's available on every ChatGPT plan"
