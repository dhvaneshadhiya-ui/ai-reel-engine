# Structure — chatgpt-chrome-reads-tabs

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer drowning in comparison tabs discovers ChatGPT can now read all of
them and come back with one answer, which matters because the tab-by-tab
copy-and-compare is the chore it removes.

## SHAPE (S17)

**Product announcement, told as one demo.** A 30s faceless cut where OpenAI's
own sofa demo IS the story: the question, the reading, the answer. The how-to
and the plan line come after the payoff, because nobody wants setup steps
before they have seen why.

## PROMISE (S2)

You will see ChatGPT answer a real shopping question by reading eight open
tabs, and learn how to do it yourself.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: sentence 1 — "Eight sofa tabs open, and one question: which one
fits my apartment?"
Paid off: "So which one fits? It comes back with seven sofas and a clear
favorite." The payoff repeats the hook's own question before answering it (S18).

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: in Chat, ChatGPT reads across your open Chrome tabs.
WHY: it compares what is on every page (dimensions, materials, prices) and
uses what it knows about you (two cats).
SO WHAT: comparison shopping across a pile of tabs becomes one question;
setup is an extension and an @Chrome mention, on every plan per OpenAI.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: OpenAI's demo with the prompt sent and the Chrome chip,
then ChatGPT's own line "I'll look through the sofa tabs you have open" — on
screen while "reading every Chrome tab you have open" is spoken.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: What does it actually do with my tabs?  A: "it checks dimensions, materials and prices on each tab"
- Q: Does the answer use anything about me?  A: "remembers you have two cats"
- Q: What did it come back with?  A: "seven sofas and a clear favorite"
- Q: How do I turn it on?  A: "add OpenAI's ChatGPT extension to Chrome, type @Chrome, and ask"
- Q: Do I need a paid plan?  A: "OpenAI says it's available on every ChatGPT plan"

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- ChatGPT acting in tabs (Work): the sibling reel's whole subject.
- The permission prompts: documented for browser control, not shown to apply to reading in Chat.
- Timing ("Worked for 25s" then 37s): two numbers on screen, neither clean.
- The two Sven Ivory tabs / 8 tabs vs 7 sofas: the dedupe reason is an inference.
- Side chat, YouTube transcripts, other browsers: older or off-title.
