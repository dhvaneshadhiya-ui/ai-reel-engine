iOS 27 gave CarPlay five new features. Four work in your car today. One, no car on the road can use yet.

First, the look: fourteen new wave wallpapers matching iOS 27, and app icons redrawn in Liquid Glass. Pick one in CarPlay's own Settings, under Wallpaper.

Second, you can finally drag the progress bar to any point in a podcast or audiobook, instead of tapping skip.

Third, Siri AI rides along. Ask it which trailhead your friend suggested, then keep talking. It needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later, and there may be a waitlist.

Fourth, Apple says wireless CarPlay is more dependable now, with sharper GPS heading, so the map knows which way you're facing.

Which leaves number five, the one I warned you about: video apps, only while you're parked. Your carmaker has to enable it, and so far none has said it will.
