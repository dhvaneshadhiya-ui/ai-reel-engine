# Research plan — ios27-carplay-features

QUESTION: What are the five CarPlay changes in iOS 27 (released 2026-09-14) that a
driver will actually notice, what does each do, what does it need, and what can we
SHOW for each?

FORMAT: top5 (list reel). Per-item subtopics 1-4 + one "is this list honest" subtopic.

Orientation (2026-09-28 search): candidates are audio scrubbing, any-app widgets on
the CarPlay dashboard, Siri AI in the car, video apps while parked, natural-language
Maps directions, more reliable wireless CarPlay/GPS, Liquid Glass wallpapers.

SUBTOPICS
1. Items A+B — audio scrubbing, third-party widgets: what/how/requirements (Apple primary)
2. Items C+D — Siri AI in CarPlay, video apps while parked: what/how/requirements, automaker support
3. Items E+F+G — Maps natural-language routing, wireless reliability/GPS, Liquid Glass look
4. Official visuals — apple.com iOS 27 page CarPlay images, WWDC26 keynote CarPlay segment timestamps, Apple newsroom stills
5. Is this list honest — which features need automaker support / which cars, what doesn't work yet (MacRumors "which actually work", pocket-lint availability), what we cut and why
