# Structure — ios27-carplay-features

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A driver who updated to iOS 27 and assumes every new CarPlay feature is now
in their car discovers that four of the five work in any CarPlay car today
and the fifth, video, needs their carmaker to switch it on (and none has
said it will), which matters because they now know what to try on their
next drive and what to stop looking for.

## SHAPE (S17)

List, with a withheld exception. Five items in order of how soon a driver
meets them (the look when CarPlay opens, the progress bar, Siri, the
connection itself), then the one that does not work yet saved for last. The
"which one?" question is what carries a viewer through a list that would
otherwise be five equal beats.

## PROMISE (S2)

Five new CarPlay features in iOS 27, and which one your car can't use yet.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: sentence 2-3, "Four work in your car today. One, no car on the
road can use yet." (count named, the exception withheld)
Paid off: item five, "And fifth, the one you can't use: video." The loop
closes on the reason (the carmaker switch) and the honest status (none has
said it will).

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: iOS 27 adds new wallpapers and icons, audio scrubbing, Siri AI, a steadier wireless
connection and video apps to CarPlay.
WHY: four are phone-side changes; video needs the car to certify it and to
report when it is parked.
SO WHAT: try the first four today (the wallpaper is picked on the car's own
screen), and don't hunt for video in a CarPlay car you already own.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: a real iOS 27 CarPlay screen (creator hands-on
footage of a car display) showing the new wave wallpaper and icons, so the
viewer sees the actual new CarPlay before the first item is named.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: How do I get the new wallpaper?  A: "Pick one in CarPlay's own Settings, under Wallpaper."
- Q: Does Siri AI work in my car?  A: "It needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later, and there may be a waitlist."
- Q: Can I watch video while driving?  A: "only while you're parked"
- Q: Why can't I find the video feature?  A: "Your carmaker has to enable it, and so far none has said it will."

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Natural-language Maps directions ("avoid tolls"): one outlet only, and it overlaps Siri.
- Widgets from any iPhone app: several round-ups call it new in iOS 27, but third-party widgets already reached CarPlay in iOS 26 (iDownloadBlog, 2025-11-24) and MacRumors' own iOS 27 guide does not list widgets. Not new, so not on the list.
- Mini player and Apple Music filters: smaller cousins of the scrubbing item.
- Route sharing to the car's own nav screen: needs car support too, would dilute the one exception.
- Voice control template for third-party apps: depends on developers, nothing visible today.
- Gemini: Apple's own release does not name it; not said.
- Siri AI language/region limits (English only, not in the EU or China): true (Apple), cut for length; the iPhone requirement and waitlist are kept as the blockers most viewers will hit.
