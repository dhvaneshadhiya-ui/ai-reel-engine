Yesterday you finally found out this phone's real name.

What nobody guessed right: not the name, not the price.

Meet the iPhone Duo, Apple's first phone that folds.

Unfolded, the screen measures 7.6 inches. Folded, it's 5.4, so it still fits like a normal iPhone.

Instead, Touch ID moved into the side button, and Face ID is gone completely.

It's also titanium, IP68 rated, running the new A20 Pro with a dual battery and a vapor chamber to keep it cool.

Nothing Apple's launched at a higher price, and this one starts at nineteen ninety-nine.

Here's the fix Apple says it chased harder than cost: a crease you genuinely can't see. Nano-texture glass, plus a titanium plate underneath the display.

Still, only Apple has actually held one. Nobody outside the company has confirmed that crease is really gone.

Remember those wrong guesses? Analysts called this thing the Ultra, or the Fold, and priced it up to three grand.

Then Apple beat every one of those guesses.

Now, this was also the first keynote from Apple's new CEO. Tim Cook handed the job to John Ternus nine days ago.

So preorders open October 16th. Could a fold win you over?
