## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer who spent the last month hearing us guess at Apple's foldable
under names that turned out wrong ("iPhone Ultra", "iPhone Fold") and
prices as high as $2,500 discovers Apple just settled every one of those
guesses at once — a real name, a real price, a real spec sheet — which
matters because it's the biggest hardware shape change to iPhone since
2007, priced higher than any iPhone before it, launched by a CEO who has
never run a keynote until today.

## SHAPE (S17)

**News / Reveal-closes-the-loop.** Not a generic product-announcement
recap — this is the payoff to three weeks of our own rumor coverage. The
shape is: state what was guessed, cut hard to what's actually confirmed,
let the mismatches (wrong name, price landed lower than the worst-case
leak, Touch ID leak that actually held up) do the work. That structure
only exists because we have real prior guesses to correct — a cold open
with no rumor framing would just be another spec-dump reel.

## PROMISE (S2)

"Here's what Apple actually announced, and where the rumors got it
wrong" — the viewer gets the real name, the real price, and the real
specs, plus which leaks turned out true and which didn't.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: opening line names the mismatch directly — the internet spent
weeks calling this phone something it isn't, and guessing a price it
doesn't have.
Paid off: closing line returns to the leaks-vs-reality frame — new CEO,
first keynote, biggest shape change since the original iPhone, and the
one number that never moved: the crease claim Apple hasn't proven yet
because nobody outside Apple has held one.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: Apple confirmed the iPhone Duo — 7.6"/5.4" folding displays, A20
Pro, titanium hinge, IP68, Touch ID in the side button, $1,999, ships
Oct 23.
WHY: this is Apple's first true shape change to the iPhone since 2007,
and it's arriving under a CEO who has never run a keynote before today.
SO WHAT: the phone itself is confirmed and buyable in six weeks — but
the thing every review is actually going to test (does the crease really
disappear) is the one claim nobody outside Apple has verified yet.

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Full storage-tier pricing ladder (256GB/512GB/1TB/2TB, up to $3,199 on
  the high end per secondary reporting) — cut because the top-end figure
  is single-sourced against Apple's own $1,999 starting price, and
  listing four price points kills momentum without adding a viewer
  decision they can act on in 75 seconds.
- Battery-life numbers (24-44hrs depending on which screen) — real and
  official, but a second numbers-block right after the price/specs
  block would flatten the pacing; the reel already spends its numeric
  budget on displays + price + release date.
- Camera system specifics (48MP Fusion Main/Ultra Wide, 2x optical) —
  cut because this reel's promise is "rumors vs. reality on the shape
  and price", not a full spec walkthrough; camera detail belongs to a
  dedicated camera-focused follow-up if the audience asks for it.
- Speculative "memory shortage drove the price up" framing from
  secondary press — cut because it's a single outlet's synthesis, not
  an Apple statement or a claim with two independent origins.
- Colors (Star White / Night Sky) and case pricing ($79/$129) — real
  but not load-bearing to the story engine; cosmetic/accessory detail.
