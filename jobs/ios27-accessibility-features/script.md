In iOS 27, point your iPhone at a bill, ask how much it is, and it reads you the total. That's one of five accessibility upgrades worth turning on, and four of them come with a catch.

That first one is Magnifier. Tap Ask, and you can ask a second question, like when the bill is due.

Two, Voice Control stops making you memorize button names. Say "tap the orange folder," and it opens.

Three, VoiceOver describes a photo in detail and answers questions about what's in it.

Four, Name Recognition alerts you when someone says your name, and it now works in more than 50 languages, including Hindi.

Five, videos with no captions now get subtitles on their own, even clips you shot, made right on the phone.

The catch? Name Recognition works on any iPhone with iOS 27. The rest run on Apple Intelligence, so you need an iPhone 15 Pro or later. And Voice Control and the subtitles are English-only, in places like the US and Canada.
