# Research plan — ios27-accessibility-features

**Question:** Which iOS 27 accessibility features are worth a viewer's attention
now that iOS 27 has shipped (2026-09-14), what does each do, how do you turn it
on, what does it need, and did it actually ship in 27.0 or is it still "later"?

**Format:** top5 (list). Lead: BGR, 2026-05-20 (José Adorno), a preview written
off Apple's May 2026 accessibility announcement — a LEAD, never a SRC. Every
claim traces to Apple (Newsroom, Support, iOS 27 feature page) or hands-on
coverage after release.

**Candidate items (7 from BGR; 5 make the list):**
1. VoiceOver Image Explorer + Live Recognition (Action button) + Magnifier with Apple Intelligence
2. Voice Control natural-language commands ("say what you see")
3. Accessibility Reader: multi-column / science / tables + translation keeping formatting
4. Auto-generated subtitles for your own videos and audio (on-device, English US/CA)
5. Name Recognition in 50+ languages (flash/notification alerts)
6. FaceTime sign-language interpreter API (developer)
7. Game controller accessibility (Sony Access Controller, combine two controllers)

## Subtopics (one agent each, parallel)

- **A — items 1 + 5:** what it does, every documented detail, how to turn it on
  (Settings path), requirements (devices, Apple Intelligence, languages), official visuals.
- **B — items 2 + 3:** same four subtopics.
- **C — items 4, 6, 7:** same four subtopics.
- **D — is this list honest + visuals:** Apple's primary May 2026 Newsroom release
  (exact wording, date); what Apple's iOS 27 page / release notes say shipped in
  27.0 vs 27.x; hands-on coverage after 2026-09-14; anything delayed, region-
  locked or Apple-Intelligence-only; what can be shown in the iOS 27 simulator
  (Settings > Accessibility panes). Download official stills to `_sources/<slug>/`.

Alternatives / "the built-in way": N/A as a subtopic — every item IS the built-in
way; agents note the iOS 26 predecessor of each feature instead (what changed).
