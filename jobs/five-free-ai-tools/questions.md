# Open questions — five-free-ai-tools

1. **The five picks.** Google AI Studio, Gemini Notebook, ElevenLabs, Suno, GitHub Copilot.
   Perplexity and DeepSeek were dropped because neither states its free limits somewhere I
   could verify (Perplexity's help centre blocks fetching; DeepSeek's site gives no numbers).
   Copilot is the most developer-leaning of the five — keep it, or swap it for something
   broader once verified?

2. **Order.** Currently AI Studio, Gemini Notebook, ElevenLabs, Suno, Copilot — roughly
   general to specialist. Gemini Notebook is arguably the most useful to most viewers and
   could lead instead.

3. **Two checker notes accepted deliberately**, because this is a list:
   - BRIDGES at 15%: each tool is a separate item; the spine is the repeated question
     ("where does free stop"), not connective tissue between tools.
   - WHAT WITHOUT SO WHAT: for four of five, the ceiling IS the meaning. Only ElevenLabs
     got an explicit "so what" ("enough for a few voiceovers, not a series").

4. **The judgement phrases.** "enough for a few voiceovers, not a series" is ours, not a
   vendor figure, and deliberately vague: no credits-to-minutes conversion is claimed
   because the pricing page states none. Happy to cut it if you would rather the reel
   carried only sourced numbers.

5. **Treatment.** Presenter bookends (hook + CTA), animated stage scenes for the five tools,
   music bed on. That means a short avatar generation for about ten seconds of face rather
   than the full runtime.
