CAPTION: Safari in iOS 27 can watch a product page for you and tell you when the price drops. 1) Notify Me: open the page, tap the menu next to the address bar, tap Notify Me and describe what you're waiting for, like "back in stock." It checks daily unless you tap Edit to change it. 2) Describe Extension: same menu. Describe what you want a site to do and Safari builds the extension. Apple's example adds a button that saves recipes. Delete one any time from Manage Extensions. 3) Tab topics: in your tabs, tap Organize, then Automatically Create Topics. Don't like it?
