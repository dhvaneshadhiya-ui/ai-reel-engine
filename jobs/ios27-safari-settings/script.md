Stop refreshing that product page, because Safari in iOS 27 will watch it for you and tell you when the price drops. That's setting one of three worth switching on, and all three share the same catch.

It's called Notify Me. Tap the menu beside the address bar, tap Notify Me, and describe what you're waiting for, like "back in stock." Safari checks daily unless you change the schedule.

That same menu holds setting two, Describe Extension. Describe what you want a site to do, and Safari builds the extension. Apple's example adds a button that saves recipes.

Setting three fixes tab clutter. In your tabs, tap Organize, then Automatically Create Topics, and Safari sorts them into groups.

Back to the catch. All three run on Apple Intelligence, which means an iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, or any iPhone 16 and up.
