# Structure — ios27-safari-settings

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

An iPhone owner who still refreshes a product page by hand discovers that
Safari in iOS 27 will watch the page for them, and that the same menu also
builds extensions from a sentence and sorts tabs into topics, which matters
because they can switch all three on tonight, as long as their iPhone runs
Apple Intelligence.

## SHAPE (S17)

List with a withheld catch. Open on the most relatable pain (refreshing a
page for a price drop) so the hook is item one's payoff, then two more items
in the order the viewer meets them: the same Page Menu (Describe Extension),
then the tab screen (topics). The catch (all three need Apple Intelligence,
an iPhone 15 Pro or later) is promised up front and paid at the end, so a
list of three equals has a reason to keep going. The Tom's Guide piece never
mentions the requirement; that gap is our added value.

## PROMISE (S2)

Three new iOS 27 Safari settings worth switching on, how to reach each one,
and the one catch they share.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: sentence 2, "and all three share the same catch."
Paid off: "Back to the catch. All three run on Apple Intelligence, which
means an iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, or any iPhone 16 and up." Then the CTA.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: Safari in iOS 27 adds Notify Me (page-change alerts), Describe
Extension (extensions written from a description) and automatic tab topics.
WHY: all three run on Apple Intelligence, which is why they sit behind the
15 Pro / iPhone 16 line.
SO WHAT: on an eligible iPhone, each is a few taps away in menus most people
never open; on an older iPhone they will not appear, so don't go looking.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook (~2-5s): Apple's own iOS 27 launch image, an iPhone 18
Pro Lock Screen showing the Safari "Notify Me" notification "An update was
detected on this page." The viewer sees the alert the hook promised.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Where do I find these?  A: "Tap the menu beside the address bar, tap Notify Me"
- Q: How often does Safari check the page?  A: "Safari checks daily unless you change the schedule."
- Q: What does Describe Extension actually make?  A: "Apple's example adds a button that saves recipes."
- Q: Can I undo the tab grouping?  A: NOT ANSWERED — cut for length; Apple's "Organize > Never" goes in the caption.
- Q: Will this work on my iPhone?  A: "All three run on Apple Intelligence, which means an iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, or any iPhone 16 and up."

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Tom's Guide's extension examples (reading-time badge, grayscale, hide distracting elements): not Apple's; Apple's own example (recipe button) used instead.
- "Local machine intelligence" / on-device: Tom's Guide only; Apple never says whether these run on device or Private Cloud Compute.
- Notify Me frequency list (hourly/daily/weekly/monthly): two outlets only; script says only "daily unless you change the schedule" (Apple's guide + screenshot).
- "Organize Tabs" label: Tom's Guide wording; Apple names the button "Organize" — script uses Apple's.
- Turning topics off (Organize > Never), managing alerts, deleting extensions: true (Apple), cut for length, go in the caption.
- Language limits (no Hindi; English India works) and China exclusion: true, one catch is enough on screen; goes in questions/caption.
- Other iOS 27 Safari features (Passwords auto-upgrade, Ask to Browse): different list.
- Limitations (Notify Me fails on some sites per iDownloadBlog): single-source hands-on; noted, not said.
