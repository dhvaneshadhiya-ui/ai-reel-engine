Apple's first foldable isn't a taller iPhone. It's a shorter, wider one.

And that one decision explains everything strange about it.

It's the iPhone Ultra. This is a leaked dummy unit — a shape mockup, not the real thing.

Unfolded, the inner screen is 7.76 inches, 4:3. That's iPad geometry, not phone geometry.

Folded, a 5.49-inch cover screen on a body so stubby people keep calling it a passport.

Apple's reportedly chasing 4.5 millimetres unfolded — the thinnest thing it has ever shipped.

Here's what that shape costs you. No depth for Face ID, so Touch ID moves to the power button.

No room for a telephoto either — two 48-megapixel cameras, and that's the whole system.

The crease, though, Apple chased regardless of cost. A liquid metal hinge, and a fold under 0.15 millimetres deep.

Anyways, nobody agrees on the price. Kuo says 2,000 to 2,500 dollars. UBS says 1,800. IDC thinks some configs touch 3,000.

And every number there is a leak. Apple has confirmed none of it.

September 9th. Would you carry a passport?
