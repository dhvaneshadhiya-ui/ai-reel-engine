# Open questions — iphone-fold-ultra

## 1. This is the third reel in a row touching this device — is the new angle right?

`iphone18-split` (Aug 13) covered "no iPhone 18, foldable instead", and
`september-preview` (Aug 14) made the iPhone Ultra its headline item and already
said 5.5"/7.8", Touch ID, dual batteries, the hinge, and $2,000–$2,500.

So this reel deliberately does **not** re-list specs. It argues one thing: Apple
built the phone **wide, not tall**, and Touch ID, the missing telephoto and the
4:3 screen all fall out of that. Say the word if you'd rather have a straight
"everything we know" spec run-through instead — it would overlap the Aug 14 reel
heavily, but it's your call.

## 2. The hero footage is a leaked DUMMY, not the phone. Confirm the framing.

Apple has shown nothing, so there is no real footage of this device. The only
honest, non-CGI material is a **non-functional shape mockup** from an
unidentified Chinese supplier, filmed by Unbox Therapy. I'm using it as the
reel's spine, with a persistent **"DUMMY UNIT · not the real device"** chip and
the Unbox Therapy credit on every beat.

I rejected the other obvious source (AppleTrack) outright: it's mostly CGI
concept renders with a webcam PiP baked in, and running a render would be
fabrication.

Fine as framed, or do you want the dummy demoted to a couple of beats?

## 3. The dummy's caliper numbers — I'm deliberately not using them.

The video measures the mockup on camera (4.60mm, 5.77mm). Those are
measurements of a *mockup*, not Apple specs, so they never appear as figures.
The caliper footage runs as texture only, and the one thickness number on screen
is the sourced **4.5mm** with a MacRumors footnote. Confirming you're happy with
that restraint — it costs a satisfying-looking number.

## 4. Price: spread, not a single figure.

Nobody agrees — Kuo $2,000–$2,500, UBS $1,800–$2,000, Fubon $2,400, IDC $2,500
average and up to $3,000. I'm showing the **disagreement** as a labelled chart
rather than picking one number.

**Settled 2026-08-17, in two steps.** First instruction: no source named in the
narration, because the spec figures are not a MacRumors exclusive and we credit
on screen anyway. Second instruction: the analyst line is fine as written —
"Kuo says 2,000 to 2,500 dollars. UBS says 1,800. IDC thinks some configs touch
3,000" — because Kuo is a well-known leak source most media cite by name.

The resulting split is the one RULES.md §3 already prescribes:

- **Aggregator (MacRumors) — never spoken.** Spec figures are stated bare
  (7.76 inches, 5.49 inches, 4.5mm, 0.15mm); the MacRumors credit lives on the
  card footnote and in the two receipts.
- **Original claimants (Kuo, UBS, IDC) — named in the line**, because they ARE
  the claim; "nobody agrees" only means something if the disagreeing parties
  are named. Chart bar labels match the names spoken.
