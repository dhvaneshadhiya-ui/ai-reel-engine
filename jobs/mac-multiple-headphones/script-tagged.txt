[curious] Your Mac can play one movie to two pairs of headphones at the same time.

It takes a free app called PairPods, and it beats Apple's own way.

[calm] First, connect every pair in Bluetooth settings.

[curious] Then open PairPods from the menu bar, tick the devices you want, and flip Share Audio on.

AirPods, Sony headphones, Samsung Buds, even a Bluetooth speaker.

Once it's on, each device gets its own volume slider, so your friend can listen louder than you.

[surprised] You also see every pair's battery and sample rate, and the crown sets the master device the others sync to.

And if a pair drops out, PairPods waits five, ten or thirty seconds for it to reconnect.

[calm] Compare Apple's route, a Multi-Output Device in Audio MIDI Setup.

It works, but it takes away your volume keys.

One catch: sync isn't guaranteed, and different models can sound slightly off-pitch, so matching pairs work best.
