## IPHONE rows, and what stands in for them

Two beats need a real device, because the simulator has no battery hardware:
**Settings → Battery** (the "recent software update is finishing" note) and
**Battery → Battery Health**. They are marked `IPHONE→GFX`: until someone records them
on a phone running iOS 27, Apple's own support page stands in, as a document, never
dressed as a screenshot of the phone. About two minutes of recording on a real iPhone
replaces both.

## Captured, perishable

The Optimising Search and Siri row was gone from the simulator within the hour of the
first capture. Everything showing it comes from the one recording that caught it.
