# Open questions — ios27-battery-longform (PILOT)

1. **This is a pilot, and it is deliberately short.** ~3 minutes against the format's
   measured 500-700s band, so G02 will advise and should. The point is to measure
   render time per minute and hear the cloned voice at length before nine minutes of
   VO credits get spent. Happy to publish it if it turns out well, or treat it as a
   test only — your call after you see it.

2. **The simulator footage.** Chapter 1 shows Apple's own iOS 27 Settings, recorded
   live from Xcode's simulator, with the "Optimising Search and Siri" row the reel
   could not show. It is genuine iOS 27 UI, and the narration never calls it "my
   phone". Fine as-is?

3. **The voice is the open question this pilot exists to answer.** 593 words is seven
   times the reel. If the clone reads flat across three minutes, the answer is the
   re-clone in references/voice-clone-recording-spec.md before any 9-minute cut.

4. **Same story as the reel, longer.** If you would rather the pilot be a NEW topic
   than a repurpose of the battery reel, say so now — the pipeline work is identical
   either way, but the repurpose costs no new research.
