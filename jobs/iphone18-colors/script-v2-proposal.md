Apple's iPhone 18 Pro has a new signature color.
It's called Dark Cherry — and the people leaking it can't agree what it looks like.
One says purplish red. Another says burgundy, coffee, and deep purple.
Which is odd, because its exact Pantone code leaked back in April.
And here's the catch: Apple's most expensive iPhone ever reportedly doesn't get it.
But first — what color is this actually?
Every render you've seen leans red.
The number says otherwise: Pantone 6076, filed under Red-Purple. In plain terms, a dark plum.
And the leaked hardware agrees — dummy units in May, a SIM tray in June, both darker and more purple than the renders.
The other three play it safe: Silver, Light Blue, and a Dark Gray that reports can't agree survives.
Now — that most expensive iPhone.
The foldable iPhone Ultra reportedly gets two: silver, and a dark blue.
Both from leaked camera protectors, not the phone.
So the boldest color in years skips the reported two-thousand-dollar foldable.
Sell millions, and a color miss gets absorbed. At that price, a color nobody wants is a warehouse full of them.
The risk goes where the volume is.
None of it's official until next month — and lighting changes how any color reads.
So — plum, not red. Would you still pick it?
