## 2. Swatches, not renders — confirm

Every "iPhone 18 Pro in Dark Cherry" image in circulation is a fan render or a
dummy model, and the reel's central claim is that those renders have the shade
wrong. Putting one on screen would contradict the narration.

So I render the actual Pantone chips from published values (6076 → #442C43,
427C → #D0D3D4, 426C → #25282A, 2121C → #8FACD9), each labeled `APPROX sRGB`
so no one reads a swatch as Apple's finish.

The two real photographs I have are the alleged Dark Cherry SIM tray and
Pantone's own 6076 C page. Both used, both credited.

**Default: swatches.**

## 5. Two checker advisories I am deliberately ignoring

- **WHAT WITHOUT SO WHAT** — the detector wants the consequence in the same
  sentence as the number. Mine is the next sentence ("So if you're picturing a
  rich red iPhone, the real one is quieter and browner than you expect"), which
  reads better aloud. The §7 translation is present, just not co-located.
- **NUMBERS one every 12 sentences** — the playbook wants one every 2-3. I cut
  the Pantone codes for the other three colors on purpose: §11 and §21 say
  prioritise momentum over density, and 6076 is the only number the story turns
  on. Padding the others back in would be density for its own sake.
