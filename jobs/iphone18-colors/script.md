Apple's iPhone 18 Pro has a new signature color.

It's called Dark Cherry — and nobody agrees what it looks like.

Which is odd, because we know its exact Pantone code.

And here's the catch: Apple's most expensive iPhone ever doesn't get it.

But first — what color is this actually?

Every render you've seen leans red.

But the leaker who detailed it says those are wrong. It's purple-tinged.

And the reports pin it to one number: Pantone 6076.

Pantone files that under Red-Purple. In plain terms: a dark plum.

So if you're picturing a rich red iPhone, the real one is quieter and browner than you expect.

It's still the only risk in the lineup: Silver, Dark Gray, and a Light Blue.

And reports don't even agree Dark Gray survives.

Now — that most expensive iPhone.

The foldable iPhone Ultra reportedly gets two. Silver, and a dark blue.

Both from leaked camera protectors, not the phone.

A bold color is a bet. Sell millions and a miss gets absorbed — but at a reported two thousand dollars and up, it's just unsold stock.

So the risk goes where the volume is.

None of it's confirmed — and lighting changes how any color reads.

So — plum, not red. Still the one you'd pick?
