# Structure — chatgpt-try-on

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

An online shopper who orders clothes, hopes, and returns half of them
discovers ChatGPT will now show the item on their own body from one selfie,
but OpenAI itself says the picture can't tell them whether it fits, and
their selfie stays saved, which matters because it changes what the feature
is good for (how it looks, not what size to buy) and what they are handing over.

## SHAPE (S17)

News, with a demonstration spine. The headline (try-on from a selfie) is shown
working in OpenAI's own screens, then the two catches OpenAI documents itself
(fit not guaranteed; selfie saved) carry the second half. News fits because
it is ten days old and the catches are what coverage skipped.

## PROMISE (S2)

You'll see how ChatGPT's new try-on works, and the one thing it still can't
tell you before you buy.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: "But it won't answer the question you'd ask right before paying."
Paid off: "And that question is: does it fit?" — returns to the hook's
question, then turns into what to do (size chart, return policy).

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: a Try on button on clothing listings; a selfie becomes a picture of you
wearing the item; screenshots from other stores work too.
WHY: it is made with ChatGPT Images, and Google already does this in Search,
so ChatGPT is catching up on shopping.
SO WHAT: use it to judge the look, not the size (OpenAI says no fit
guarantee); your selfie is saved, here is where to delete it.

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: OpenAI's official screen of the result, the woman
wearing the denim skirt on the product page, with the Try on button visible
(openai_Virtual-Try-On.webp, right phone, cropped 9:16), then pull to the
listing with the Try on button.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: How do I use it?  A: "Tap it, take or upload a selfie, and ChatGPT makes a picture of you wearing it"
- Q: Will it tell me my size?  A: "OpenAI says try-on doesn't guarantee fit or size"
- Q: What happens to my photo?  A: "saved for your next try-on. To remove it, go to Settings, Personalization, Reference photos"
- Q: Does it work for clothes from other shops?  A: "Upload a screenshot and ask ChatGPT to put it on you"
- Q: Is it on my phone?  A: "on mobile and web"

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- "Global launch": only TechCrunch says it; OpenAI names no regions.
- Plan tiers: undocumented.
- ChatGPT Images 2.5: the version is TechCrunch's, not OpenAI's.
- Training on photos: an inference across two pages, not stated for try-on.
- Instant Checkout's scale-back: true context, but a second story in 60s.
- Favorites folders / Library: on screen, not spoken.
- Retail Dive's conversion stat: vendor survey, not about ChatGPT.
