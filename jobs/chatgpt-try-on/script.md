ChatGPT can now show you wearing clothes you haven't bought yet. All it needs is one selfie. But it won't answer the question you'd ask right before paying.

Open a clothing or accessory listing in ChatGPT and you'll find a new Try on button. Tap it, take or upload a selfie, and ChatGPT makes a picture of you wearing it.

Spotted something in another store? Upload a screenshot and ask ChatGPT to put it on you.

OpenAI added it on October first, on mobile and web, and it's made with ChatGPT Images. Google already does this in Search, so ChatGPT is catching up on shopping.

And that question is: does it fit? OpenAI says try-on doesn't guarantee fit or size, so before you check out, you still need the size chart and the return policy.

Your selfie, meanwhile, is saved for your next try-on. To remove it, go to Settings, Personalization, Reference photos.

If you're not ready to buy yet, save the item to Favorites and come back to it later.
