# Research plan — chatgpt-try-on (2026-10-12)

QUESTION: What exactly did OpenAI ship with ChatGPT's virtual try-on, how does a
viewer use it, what can't it do, and what are the catches (privacy, fit, availability)?
FORMAT: news

1. Primary source — OpenAI's announcement / help-center page: exact wording, date, availability (plans, regions, mobile/web), how Try On works step by step.
2. Independent confirmation — two domains that did not copy each other (TechCrunch, Engadget, The Verge...) with VIA.
3. Context — what changed vs before (ChatGPT shopping history, Instant Checkout discontinued, Favorites), Google's try-on as precedent, numbers.
4. Reaction — reviewers' hands-on impressions, accuracy, users, competitors.
5. Contradict it — limitations, privacy (reference photos stored, training opt-out), fit accuracy, which items/retailers are supported, any rollout gaps.
6. Official visuals — OpenAI blog images/video, help-center screenshots, social posts; what each shows; mobile captures possible.
