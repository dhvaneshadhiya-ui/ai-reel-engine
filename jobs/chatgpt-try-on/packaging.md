TITLE: ChatGPT Can Now Put Clothes On You
CAPTION: On October 1, OpenAI added virtual try-on to ChatGPT. On a clothing or accessory listing in ChatGPT's shopping results, tap Try on, then take or upload a selfie, and ChatGPT generates a picture of you wearing the item. You can also upload a picture of clothing in a conversation, like a screenshot from another store, and ask ChatGPT to show it on you. It works on mobile and web and is made with ChatGPT Images. Google already offers AI try-on in Search. OpenAI's help page says try-on images may not represent the product or your appearance exactly and do not guarantee fit or size, so check the merchant's measurements and return policy before buying. Your reference photo is saved for future try-ons; to change or remove it, go to Settings > Personalization > Reference photos. A new Favorites option saves products to your ChatGPT Library.
