## FEATURES + HOW TO USE

OpenAI-documented features (release note Oct 1 2026 + shopping help article):
- Try on button on clothing/accessory listings in shopping results — USED (the spine)
- Take or upload a selfie as the reference photo — USED
- Generated try-on image made with ChatGPT Images — USED
- Upload a picture/screenshot of clothing in a conversation and ask to try it on — USED
- Reference photo saved for future try-ons; change or delete in Settings > Personalization > Reference photos — USED
- Favorites: save any product (bookmark icon) — USED
- Folders inside Favorites, browse in ChatGPT Library — CUT (detail; Favorites line covers it, shown on screen)
- Product results "are not ads" — CUT (true, but a different story; would raise a question the reel doesn't answer)
- Same-day camera "Scan" (iOS) — CUT (separate feature, would muddle)

Vendor how-to steps (help article), in order:
1. Open a product listing for clothes or accessories in ChatGPT — USED
2. Select the "Try on" button — USED
3. Take or upload a selfie — USED
4. View your generated try-on image — USED
5. (Alt) upload a clothing picture in a conversation and ask ChatGPT to show it on you — USED

## NOT CLAIMED

- "Global launch" / "available everywhere": only TechCrunch says it (VIA OpenAI's briefing); OpenAI's pages list no regions. Canada confirmed first-hand. NOT spoken.
- Which plans get it (Free/Plus/Pro): not stated by OpenAI. NOT spoken.
- "ChatGPT Images 2.5": TechCrunch's attribution; OpenAI says only "ChatGPT Images".
- Full-body photo: TechCrunch and Android Authority mention it; OpenAI's pages say selfie. Script says selfie.
- Shoes, glasses, jewelry specifically: OpenAI says "clothes and accessories" only. On-screen items stay to clothes/purse shown in OpenAI's own stills.
- That reference photos are used to train models: inference across two separate OpenAI pages; not stated for try-on. NOT spoken.
- How long photos are kept: not published.
- Named retailers, merchant opt-out, accuracy data, usage numbers: none published.
- Instant Checkout "discontinued": it was scaled back/deprioritised (Mar 2026). Cut from the script.
- Retail Dive's "more than double" conversion: a 2024 vendor survey, not about ChatGPT.
- OpenAI's demo brand "Curbloom" and the model in the stills are demo content, never presented as real.
