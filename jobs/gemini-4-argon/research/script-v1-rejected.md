Google's newest AI, Gemini 4 Argon, spent its first two weeks with cyber defenders only. Now it's yours too, if you pay, and Google had a reason for that order.

Argon is Google's new flagship, and it's live in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

The reason is security. Google says Argon can find security holes, test them and patch them by itself. So defenders got the first look, and your version refuses to help with attacks.

For everything else, it can read about a million tokens in one go, and it takes in text, images, video, audio and PDFs. But it only answers in text, so it won't make images or audio.

As for how good it is, the independent Artificial Analysis index has it tied with OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra. But Bloomberg reports some Google staff say it struggles with real coding work, which Google disputes.

And if you build with it, it's two dollars per million input tokens until November thirtieth. After that, the price doubles.
