OpenAI just announced ChatGPT ads that show up while your picture is still loading. And on Free, the way out costs you the picture.

In OpenAI's own example, the image is sixty-two percent done, and right under it is a grocery ad, with photos and a Learn more button.

ChatGPT reaches one point two billion people a week, and ads have run under its answers since February. Now they get pictures, showing a product or how you'd use it.

Testing starts later this month in the US, during image generation only. OpenAI says the ads are labeled, kept separate from your image, and don't change ChatGPT's answers.

Paying for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise or Edu? You get no ads. Free and Go users do, unless they're under eighteen.

The way out is in Settings, under Ads controls. But OpenAI's help page says it costs you messages, and image generation. So if you make images on Free, the ads come with them.
