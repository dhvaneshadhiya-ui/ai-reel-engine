# Structure — openai-image-ads

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`frameworks/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A free ChatGPT user who thinks ads live under text answers discovers they
are coming to the picture they are waiting on, and that the free no-ads
mode removes image generation entirely, which matters because on the free
plan, making images and skipping ads stop being compatible.

## SHAPE (S17)

News, with a twist ending. The announcement itself is advertiser-facing
(measurement partners, CPA stats); what a viewer cares about is where the ad
sits, who sees it, and how to avoid it. The hook plants the trap, the body
explains the format and who is exempt, the ending springs the trap.

## PROMISE (S2)

Where the new ad shows up, who sees it, and why the free way out costs you
the image tool.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: hook, "And on Free, the way out costs you the picture."
Paid off: "The way out is in Settings ... OpenAI's help page says it costs
you messages, and image generation. So if you make images on Free, the ads
come with them."

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: a visual ad format, tested during image generation, US, later in October.
WHY: OpenAI says pictures show a product, its use, or the experience; ads
already run under answers since February; 1.2 billion weekly users.
SO WHAT: Free and Go users get them; on Free the only no-ads option has no
image generation, so free image-making now comes with ads (in test regions).

## CONFIRMATION BEAT (2-5s)

Right after the hook: OpenAI's own mockup — the loading image at 62% with
the Heirloom Grocery ad and "Learn more" button directly under it, pushed in
on one phone so it fills the frame.

## VIEWER QUESTIONS

- Q: Where exactly does the ad show up?  A: "the image is sixty-two percent done, and right under it is a grocery ad"
- Q: Will I see it?  A: "Paying for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise or Edu? You get no ads. Free and Go users do"
- Q: Is it live now?  A: "Testing starts later this month in the US"
- Q: Does it change ChatGPT's answers?  A: "the ads are labeled, kept separate from your image, and don't change ChatGPT's answers"
- Q: Can I turn it off for free?  A: "OpenAI's help page says it costs you messages, and image generation"

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Measurement partners (Hightouch, AppsFlyer, etc.) and DV/IAS brand-safety pilots: advertiser plumbing.
- WeightWatchers -15.3% CPA, Dose 67%, Portland Leather 93%: advertiser case studies, irrelevant to a user.
- Negative Phrases for advertisers: advertiser-facing.
- Under-18 age prediction details, Temporary Chats have no ads, Ad controls (personalization off): useful but would dilute the twist; Temporary Chats kept as a possible packaging note.
- Dec 2025 "promotional messages" backlash: a different thing, would muddle.
