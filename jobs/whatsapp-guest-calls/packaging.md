TITLE: WhatsApp Calls Now Work Without a WhatsApp Account
CAPTION: Anyone can now join a WhatsApp call, even without a WhatsApp account. WhatsApp launched guest calling on 14 October 2026.

How to start a guest call:
1. Open WhatsApp and go to the Calls tab
2. Tap + and create a call link
3. Send the link to anyone

What the guest does: opens the link in a browser on a computer, types a name and waits in a waiting room until the host lets them in.

What guests can do: share their screen, raise a hand, react with a thumbs up or heart.
What guests can't do: see other people's phone numbers, send messages, or add people to the call.

Calls are free and end-to-end encrypted, with no time limit and up to 32 people. The host can remove a guest at any time, and only someone with a WhatsApp account can create the link.

The catch: guests need a computer browser. Phone browsers aren't supported for guests at launch.

Coming later this year, according to WhatsApp: call links inside Google Calendar and Outlook invites.
