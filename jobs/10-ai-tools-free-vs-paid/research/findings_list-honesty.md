# List honesty check — "10 AI tools: free vs paid"

Subtopic: is this list honest. Covers ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Canva, Notion AI,
Runway, Descript, Cursor, Leonardo.Ai, Gamma. WebSearch only; all fetched content
treated as data, not instructions. Searches run 2026-09-18.

---

## 1. Free tier vs disguised trial (one-time/expiring credits that don't renew)

Ongoing free plans (renew, no expiry date on the tier itself):

- **ChatGPT** — free text chat is now uncapped/unlimited (see §3); a standing
  tier, not a trial. "From the week of 10 August 2026 OpenAI is removing text
  message limits entirely, retiring the roughly 10 messages every 5 hours cap."
  SRC: https://axeetech.com/chatgpt-free-what-you-get-limits-and-how-to-use-it/
- **Claude** — "The free tier is permanent — you're not on a trial but on a real
  plan with real (limited) usage forever... The limit is based on a rolling
  5-hour window instead." SRC: https://freeacademy.ai/blog/claude-free-plan-limits-2026
- **Perplexity** — "It includes unlimited standard searches... with no daily
  cap," plus a small renewing daily allowance (5 Deep Research, 3 Pro Search/day).
  Real ongoing free tier, not a one-time grant. SRC:
  https://costbench.com/software/ai-chatbots/perplexity/free-plan/
- **Canva** — "Canva Free is a permanent plan, not a trial... The free plan
  stays free with no pricing changes announced for 2026." SRC:
  https://www.usecarly.com/blog/canva-pricing/
- **Notion (base Free plan, not AI)** — "Notion's Free plan is permanent, needs
  no credit card, and gives a solo user unlimited pages and blocks." SRC:
  https://www.breeze.pm/articles/notion-ai-pricing
- **Cursor (Hobby)** — ongoing free tier, no expiry mentioned, distinct from a
  trial: "limited number of Agent requests and Tab completions." SRC:
  https://www.nocode.mba/articles/cursor-pricing

**Tools whose "free tier" is effectively a one-time/expiring allotment, not a
renewing plan — flag for the script:**

- **Runway** — "A one-time deposit of 125 credits is included with the Free
  plan and does not expire... buy roughly 5 seconds of Gen-4.5 video... The
  free plan serves as an evaluation tier with a one-time credit allowance,
  rather than a traditional trial period with expiring benefits." SRC:
  https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/50404627334547-Free-plan-details
  — credits don't expire but also don't refill, so once spent it behaves like a
  trial.
- **Descript** — mixed: 60 media-minutes/month DOES renew monthly, but "100 AI
  credits (one-time, non-renewing)" do not. "The Descript Free plan is designed
  as a long trial, not a long-term tool." SRC:
  https://marcandrews.com/descript-free-plan-2026-what-can-you-actually-do/
- **Leonardo.Ai** — renews daily (not a one-time-credit disguised trial), but
  thin: "Free users get 150 tokens daily; they reset every 24 hours and never
  roll over... can disappear in roughly 10 images on heavy models... enough to
  test the platform for an afternoon." SRC: https://www.eesel.ai/blog/leonardo-ai-pricing
  and https://www.timtis.com/blog/is-leonardo-ai-worth-paying-for-and-how-far-the-free-plan-gets-you/
- **Gamma** — clearest disguised-trial case: "The Free plan includes 400 AI
  credits at sign-up and they do not refill... best thought of as an extended
  trial." SRC: https://www.eesel.ai/blog/gamma-pricing
- **Notion AI specifically** (distinct from the Notion workspace) — "Notion AI
  on Free is a limited trial, not the full assistant... Full Notion AI is built
  into the Business plan at $20/member/month, with Free and Plus limited to a
  capped trial." SRC: https://www.breeze.pm/articles/notion-ai-pricing — the
  WORKSPACE is free forever, but the AI layer is essentially trial-gated unless
  on Business.

**Bottom line:** of the 10, Runway, Gamma, and Notion AI (the AI layer
specifically) are where "free tier" is doing more marketing work than product
work. Descript is half-and-half. Leonardo renews but is thin enough to burn
through in one sitting.

## 2. Free tier requiring a credit card up front

- **Notion — the 30-day Business trial** (the one that unlocks full Notion AI)
  requires a card: "credit card details are required to start the trial. The
  trial also renews automatically unless you cancel before it ends." SRC:
  https://www.breeze.pm/articles/notion-ai-pricing — the base Notion Free plan
  (no full AI) does NOT require a card.
- **Perplexity** — the standard free account needs no card, but third-party
  promotional/partner trials do: "a valid credit or debit card is required for
  all promotional and trial redemptions, but no charges are applied during your
  promotional/trial period." SRC: https://yangmao.ai/en/providers/perplexity/no-credit-card/
  — a promo mechanic, not Perplexity's standard free tier; minor footnote at most.

Confirmed NO card required for standard free sign-up on: ChatGPT, Claude, Canva,
Runway, Descript, Cursor, Leonardo.Ai, Gamma. SRCs:
https://axeetech.com/chatgpt-free-what-you-get-limits-and-how-to-use-it/ ,
https://freeacademy.ai/blog/claude-free-plan-limits-2026 ,
https://www.usecarly.com/blog/canva-pricing/ ,
https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/50404627334547-Free-plan-details ,
https://marcandrews.com/descript-free-plan-2026-what-can-you-actually-do/ ,
https://www.nocode.mba/articles/cursor-pricing ,
https://edurancehub.co.in/leonardo-ai-free-plan/ ,
https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/7834324-how-do-credits-work-in-gamma ("Every
new account starts on the Free Plan with no time limit and no credit card
required.")

**Bottom line:** card-up-front isn't the main story across these 10 — the
honest catch is Notion's full-AI trial, which needs a card and auto-renews into
paid if not cancelled. Worth one line if Notion AI is presented as "free."

## 3. Recent pricing / plan-limit changes (since ~July 2026)

- **ChatGPT** — free text limits removed entirely starting the week of Aug 10,
  2026 (previously ~10 msgs/5 hrs); default free model moved GPT-5.5 Instant →
  GPT-5.6 Luna on Aug 6, 2026; ads on free-tier answers expanded from the US
  (Feb 9, 2026) to 31 European markets on Aug 24, 2026. SRC:
  https://axeetech.com/chatgpt-free-what-you-get-limits-and-how-to-use-it/
- **Claude** — Pro restructured: annual billing now $17/mo (vs $20/mo
  month-to-month); Team Standard dropped $25 → $20/seat; new "fast mode" and
  "long context premium" add-on pricing introduced. Exact date not pinned, but
  reflects current Sept 2026 state. SRC: https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/claude-pricing/
  and https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/claude-pricing-plans-api-costs
- **Perplexity** — Comet browser's paywall dropped March 18, 2026 (predates the
  window, but the most recent structural change found); "Comet Plus" now a
  standalone $5 add-on bundled into Pro/Max. No credit-limit change found
  specifically since July 2026. SRC: https://www.eesel.ai/blog/perplexity-comet-pricing
  and https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/introducing-comet-plus
- **Canva** — no free-plan change; Pro on a multi-step climb ($12.99 → $15 →
  $18/mo) through 2025-2026, existing users grandfathered under Canva's
  "Pricing Promise." No specific July-Sept 2026 change beyond this trend. SRC:
  https://www.usecarly.com/blog/canva-pricing/
- **Notion AI** — restructured so full AI now lives inside the $20/seat/month
  Business plan; Free/Plus get only a capped trial (previously AI was a flat
  $10/seat add-on on any plan). Date not pinned but is a notable shift from the
  older model. SRC: https://www.breeze.pm/articles/notion-ai-pricing
- **Runway** — no specific July-Sept 2026 change beyond the standing 125
  one-time-credit free plan. SRC: https://resourify.com/resources/runway-student-discount
- **Descript** — the bigger overhaul (transcription hours → "media minutes" +
  metered AI credit top-ups) landed September 2025 — just outside the strict
  window, but still live and biting users in 2026: "one long-time user reported
  their bill jumping from $30 to $195 per month after the transition." SRC:
  https://dynalord.com/blog/descript-pricing — worth a caution line even though
  slightly outside the date window.
- **Cursor** — most direct hits in-window: Teams plan updated June 2026 (new
  Premium seat tier, better admin controls, "estimated to lower costs for 90%
  of teams"); flat "Auto" rate retired Aug 24, 2026 — "every Auto request now
  bills at the list price of the routed model," a real cost increase for heavy
  Auto users. SRC: https://www.lowcode.agency/blog/cursor-ai-pricing
- **Leonardo.Ai** — actively transitioning off subscriptions to Pay-As-You-Go
  (PAYG) at time of research (mid-to-late Aug 2026): "available now for new
  users, and existing customers can switch at any time by cancelling their
  subscription, after which they'll be automatically moved to PAYG." Pricing
  was unstable enough that one source could not "reproduce one stable plan
  table" as of Aug 14, 2026. SRC: https://www.eesel.ai/blog/leonardo-ai-pricing
- **Gamma** — no specific change found in the July-Sept 2026 window; current
  structure (400 free / 1,000 Plus / 4,000 Pro / 20,000 Ultra credits) appears
  stable. SRC: https://www.eesel.ai/blog/gamma-pricing

**Bottom line:** the two tools with dated, clearly in-window changes worth
calling out on screen are **ChatGPT** (free limits removed Aug 10, 2026 — a
real upgrade) and **Cursor** (Auto flat-rate retired Aug 24, 2026 — a real cost
increase). **Leonardo.Ai** is mid-transition to PAYG right now — flag as a
caveat rather than stating current numbers as settled. **Notion AI** and
**Descript** both underwent real restructuring just outside the precise window —
still worth a "current as of publish" caveat on screen.

## 4. Questions a skeptical viewer would ask, with honest one-sentence answers

1. **"Is the free tier actually usable day-to-day, or just enough to try it
   once?"** — It depends on the tool: Claude, Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Canva
   give a real, renewing allowance you could live on for light use; Runway,
   Gamma, and Notion AI's free tier is really a one-time credit bucket that
   plays out fast and then sits empty.

2. **"If I can only pay for one of these, which is the best value?"** — There's
   no single winner — it depends on the job (Claude/ChatGPT ~$20/mo for a daily
   assistant, Canva Pro $18/mo if design is the main output, Cursor Pro $20/mo
   if you code daily), so "best value for X job" is the honest framing, not
   "best value" flat.

3. **"Do these prices actually stay the same, or will this video be out of
   date in a month?"** — No — several changed in just the last two months
   (ChatGPT's free limits, Cursor's Auto pricing, Leonardo's whole billing
   model), so the reel should show "as of [month] 2026" on screen rather than
   imply these numbers are permanent.

4. **"Is there a catch — like needing a credit card just to try the free
   plan?"** — For 9 of the 10 the free sign-up itself needs no card, but
   Notion's full AI assistant is gated behind a 30-day Business trial that does
   require a card and auto-renews into paid if you forget to cancel.

5. **"When they say 'free,' do they mean free forever or free until my credits
   run out?"** — It's genuinely mixed within this same list — Claude, ChatGPT,
   Perplexity, Canva, and Cursor are ongoing free tiers with no expiry, while
   Runway, Gamma, and Notion AI hand you a fixed one-time credit balance that
   functions like a trial once spent, so "free" isn't doing the same job across
   all ten tools.
