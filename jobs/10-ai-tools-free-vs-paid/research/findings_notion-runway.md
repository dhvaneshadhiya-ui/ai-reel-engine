# Findings — Notion AI & Runway (subtopic 3)

Researched 2026-09-18. Sourced from official pricing/help pages, fetched
directly (not search snippets). Both tools had a real structural pricing
change in the last few months — flag as noted below, do not state as
permanently fixed.

## NOTION AI

- CLAIM: Notion AI is no longer sold as a standalone add-on — full Notion AI
  is now bundled only into the Business plan and above.
  SRC: https://www.notion.com/pricing (fetched 2026-09-18)
  QUOTE: Business plan ($20/month per member) description lists AI features
  ("Notion Agent... Completes complex, multi-step tasks using context from
  Notion, your connected apps, and the web", "AI Meeting Notes", "Enterprise
  Search - Beta") as included; Free ($0) and Plus ($10/month per member) are
  described only as "Trial AI capabilities like generating docs, or
  autofilling databases."
  RECENT CHANGE - HEDGE: third-party trackers (not fetched as primary, treat
  as VIA/single-source) date this restructure to ~May 2025, when the flat
  $10/member/month AI add-on was folded into Business. Older than "last 1-2
  months," so reasonably stable, but Notion's pricing page itself gives no
  change-log date, so don't claim an exact date on screen.

- CLAIM: Free and Plus plans get a capped number of free AI responses, but
  Notion's own help page does NOT publish the exact number.
  SRC: https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-faqs (fetched 2026-09-18)
  QUOTE (official): "Users on the Free and Plus Plans get a limited number of
  complimentary AI responses so they can try Notion AI features out." — no
  digit given on this official page.
  VIA (single/unverified, third-party blogs, NOT official — do not put this
  number in the script without hedging or re-verifying): multiple SEO/review
  sites (coworker.ai, costbench.com, aitoolgrade.com — surfaced via
  WebSearch, not fetched directly) claim "~20 free AI responses" per
  member/workspace. TIER: single/disputed. If used on screen, must be spoken
  hedged (e.g. "roughly 20") per framework S20, and ideally re-confirmed by
  signing into a live free Notion workspace before air.

- CLAIM: Business plan costs $20/month per member and is the first tier with
  full Notion AI (Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, Enterprise Search beta),
  plus 30-day data retention.
  SRC: https://www.notion.com/pricing (fetched 2026-09-18)
  QUOTE: "Business Plan ($20/month per member)... Does work for you.
  Completes complex, multi-step tasks... Automate your meeting notes and
  follow-ups, no bot needed... Search across connected apps like Slack,
  Github & more... 30-day retention."

- CLAIM: Enterprise plan is custom-priced and adds zero-data-retention for AI
  processing on top of the same Agent/Meeting Notes/Search feature set.
  SRC: https://www.notion.com/pricing (fetched 2026-09-18)
  QUOTE: "When using Notion AI, our LLM providers utilize zero data
  retention" (listed as an Enterprise-tier line).

- CLAIM: There's also a separate, newer "Custom Agents" add-on billed by
  usage credits, distinct from the core AI features above.
  SRC: https://www.notion.com/pricing (fetched 2026-09-18)
  QUOTE: "Free to try, then $10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits."
  Note: not clearly attached to a specific plan tier on the page as fetched —
  worth a follow-up look at notion.com/product/agents before scripting this
  line; wasn't the assigned subtopic focus and wasn't verified further here.

- LOGO SOURCE (not downloaded, URL only): no first-party notion.com
  brand-assets/press-kit page surfaced in this search pass. The only source

  FLAG: unlike Runway, no first-party Notion brand page confirmed here —
  verify via Notion's own site footer/help center for an official brand page

## RUNWAY

- CLAIM: Free plan gives 125 one-time credits (not monthly), plus 5GB
  storage.
  SRC: https://runway.com/pricing (fetched 2026-09-18, redirected from
  runwayml.com/pricing)
  QUOTE: "125 one-time credits to explore Runway's AI tools."

- CLAIM: Standard plan (cheapest paid tier) is $12/month billed annually
  ($15/month billed monthly), includes 625 credits/month (7,500/year), no
  watermark, 5 parallel generations, 3 projects, 20GB storage — enough for
  roughly 52s of Gen-4.5 video or 104s of Gen-4 Turbo, or 78 Gen-4 images
  (1080p).
  SRC: https://runway.com/pricing (fetched 2026-09-18)
  QUOTE: "Standard... $12/month [billed annually]... regular price $15/month
  ('Save $36/year')... 625/month = 7,500/year credits... 52s of Gen-4.5, 104s
  of Gen-4 Turbo... 78 Gen-4 Images (1080p)... No watermarks... 20GB
  storage."

- CLAIM: Pro plan is $28/month annual ($35/month monthly), 2,250 credits/mo
  (27,000/yr) — ~187s Gen-4.5 / 375s Gen-4 Turbo video, 281 Gen-4 images,
  15 parallel generations, 5 projects, 1 Brand Kit, 1 custom voice clone,
  unlimited 4K upscaling, 100GB storage.
  SRC: https://runway.com/pricing (fetched 2026-09-18)
  QUOTE: "Pro... $28/month... 2,250/month = 27,000/year... 187s of Gen-4.5,
  375s of Gen-4 Turbo... 281 Gen-4 Images (1080p)... 'Unlimited 4K video
  upscaling'."

- CLAIM: Top consumer/prosumer tier is now called "Max" (renamed from what
  was "Pro" before a spring-2026 restructure), $76/month annual ($95/month
  monthly), 9,500 credits/mo (114,000/yr), 20 parallel generations, 10
  projects, 3 Brand Kits, 3 voice clones, early access to new models,
  500GB storage, studio-grade HDR/ProRes output.
  SRC: https://runway.com/pricing (fetched 2026-09-18)
  QUOTE: "Max... $76/month... 9,500/month = 114,000/year... 'Early access to
  new models'... 'Studio-grade quality generations: HDR, ProRes'."

- RECENT CHANGE - HEDGE HARD ON SCREEN: Runway's plan names and structure
  moved within the last few months, so don't present today's numbers as
  though they've been stable. Per WebSearch results (VIA — third-party
  coverage, e.g. eesel.ai, aumiqx.com, creatify.ai; not independently
  fetched/verified against a Runway changelog page):
  - Runway retired the old "Unlimited" tier; renamed old "Basic"->"Standard",
    old "Standard"->"Pro", old "Pro"->"Max" (Max's credit allowance raised
    from 6,750 to 9,500) in spring 2026.
  - Existing "Unlimited" subscribers were migrated to "Max" on September 1,
    2026 — i.e. within the last 2-3 weeks of this research date
    (2026-09-18). This is squarely inside the "changed in the last 1-2
    months" window — script language should say "as of September 2026" and
    avoid implying these tier names/prices are long-established.
  TIER: single/multi (several independent trackers agree on the shape, none
  is Runway's own changelog) — treat the migration DATE as solid enough to
  speak, but hedge the exact "was $X, now $Y" history since it wasn't
  confirmed on a first-party Runway page.
