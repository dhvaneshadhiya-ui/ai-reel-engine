# Research plan — 10-ai-tools-free-vs-paid

## Research question
For each of 10 AI tools, what does the free tier actually include (numbers,
features), and what does the paid tier cost and specifically unlock on top of
it — as of September 2026, from the tool's own pricing page?

## Format
`ai-tools` (list/round-up, 10 items). Per the list/top-N recipe: subtopics 1-4
condensed to PRICE + FREE LIMITS + PAID UNLOCK + LOGO/VISUAL per item (one
subagent covers 2 tools), plus one "is this list honest" pass.

## Subtopics

1. ChatGPT (OpenAI) + Claude (Anthropic) — free vs paid, pricing pages,
   official logo source
2. Perplexity + Canva (Magic Studio / AI features) — free vs paid, pricing
   pages, official logo source
3. Notion AI + Runway — free vs paid, pricing pages, official logo source
4. Descript + Cursor — free vs paid, pricing pages, official logo source
5. Leonardo.Ai + Gamma — free vs paid, pricing pages, official logo source
6. Is this list honest — any tool whose free tier is effectively unusable
   (trial-in-disguise), any price that changed in the last 1-2 months and is
   likely to move again, and the 3-5 viewer questions this reel should answer
