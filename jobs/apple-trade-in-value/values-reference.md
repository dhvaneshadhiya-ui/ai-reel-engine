## Two things worth knowing before you answer

**The US floor is a real model.** Apple's headline reads "$40–$720", and $40 is
exactly the iPhone 8 and 8 Plus ceiling. The range is not rhetorical: it runs
from the oldest eligible iPhone at its best to the newest Pro Max at its best.

**The India floor is NOT in this table.** India's headline reads
"₹4500.00–₹57000.00", but the lowest iPhone listed is the iPhone 8 at ₹5,500.
So ₹4,500 belongs to something not on this list, and I could not verify what.
Do not attribute it to a model when you answer — say the range starts there and
leave it.

## India is a different offer, not a translation

- It is branded **"iPhone Exchange Offer"**, and the page title is "Exchange",
  not "Trade In".
- The headline ties credit to a purchase: *"Buy a new iPhone today and get
  ₹4500.00–₹57000.00 credit when you exchange."* The US page does not require a
  purchase in its headline.
- India runs a separate Android line: *"Buy an iPhone today and get
  ₹3000.00–₹37000.00 credit when you exchange an Android device."* There is no
  equivalent on the US page.
- iPhone 16 Plus and iPhone 16 are the SAME ₹36,000 in India; in the US they
  differ ($485 vs $480).
