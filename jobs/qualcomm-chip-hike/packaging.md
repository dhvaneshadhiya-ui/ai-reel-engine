## youtube

CAPTION: Your next Android phone is about to cost more, and it started today.

Qualcomm raised Snapdragon prices from September 1, 2026, by double digits — confirmed by CEO Cristiano Amon on the Q3 FY2026 earnings call and to CNBC. It covers phone chips, wearables and Arm laptop chips.

But the chip increase is the small number. Qualcomm's own release blames input costs "across wafer fabrication, assembly, test, advanced packaging, memory and other materials" — and memory is the one doing the work. TrendForce projected conventional DRAM contract prices rising 58-63% quarter on quarter in 2Q26. J.P. Morgan puts DRAM up more than 400% between the start of 2024 and the end of 2026, driven by AI data-centre demand.

Qualcomm unveils its next flagship chip at Snapdragon Summit in Maui on September 22-24. That is the first silicon sold at the new price, and it goes into 2027 phones. No phone maker has announced a retail increase.

Sources: Qualcomm Q3 FY2026 earnings call - CNBC - 9to5Google - TrendForce - J.P. Morgan Global Research - Qualcomm.com. Footage: Qualcomm, Micron Technology.
