TITLE: Snapdragon Price Hike Explained: Why Your Next Android Phone Costs More
CAPTION: Your next Android phone is about to cost more, and it started today.

Qualcomm raised Snapdragon prices from September 1, 2026, by double digits. CEO Cristiano Amon confirmed it on the Q3 FY2026 earnings call and to CNBC: "Cost went up, prices are going to go up." Reporting on the customer letter puts phone chips, wearables and Windows-on-Arm laptop chips in scope.

But the chip increase is the small number. Qualcomm's own release blames a broad-based rise in input costs "across wafer fabrication, assembly, test, advanced packaging, memory and other materials" — and memory is the one doing the work. TrendForce projected conventional DRAM contract prices rising 58-63% quarter on quarter in 2Q26. J.P. Morgan puts DRAM up more than 400% between the start of 2024 and the end of 2026, driven by AI data-centre demand.

Qualcomm unveils its next flagship chip at Snapdragon Summit in Maui on September 22-24. That is the first silicon sold at the new price, and it goes into 2027 phones, watches and Arm laptops.

No phone maker has announced a retail increase. Qualcomm expects the benefit in its own gross margins within a couple of quarters. Yours lands a lot later.
