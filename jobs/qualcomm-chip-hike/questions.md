# Open questions — qualcomm-chip-hike

1. **The CTA is a question, not a comment-gate.** "Buying now, or waiting it
   out?" invites replies without asking anyone to type a keyword. `news` does
   not require a CTA at all, so the alternative is to end on "Yours lands much
   later." and stop. Want the question, or the harder stop?

2. **Two figures were deliberately left out, and both are tempting.** The
   Galaxy S27 Ultra price leaks ($1,399 / $1,499) and the leaked ~$300 cost of
   the next Snapdragon are the most clickable numbers in this story, and both
   are leak-tier. Putting either in would mean speaking a rumour with the same
   confidence as an earnings call. Happy to add one with an explicit "leaked"
   frame if you want the extra bite.

3. **"AI data centers" is the cause, stated plainly.** TrendForce and J.P.
   Morgan both say it; Qualcomm does not. The script never puts that sentence
   in Qualcomm's mouth. Say so more loudly, or leave it as it is?

4. **The reel does not tell anyone what to do.** No "buy now before prices
   rise". That would be advice we cannot support — nobody has confirmed a
   retail increase. Confirming you want it to stay a report.
