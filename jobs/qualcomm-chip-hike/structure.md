# Structure — qualcomm-chip-hike

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`styles/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer who assumes phone prices rise because of tariffs or greed discovers
that the chip in almost every premium Android device got more expensive today
— and that the chipmaker's own double-digit rise is the SMALL number, because
AI data centres bought the memory. It matters because the phone they buy in
2027 is already more expensive, and nobody in a shop will ever tell them why.

## SHAPE (S17)

**News → Explainer.** The news half is one line long (prices up, today, double
digits) because it is genuinely one line. Everything after it is the explainer
that the headline coverage skipped: WHAT went up → WHY (input costs) → the
real cause underneath (memory, AI) → SO WHAT (which phones, and when).
Chosen over a straight News shape because the news alone changes nothing a
viewer can act on — the mechanism is the value.

## PROMISE (S2)

You will understand why an Android phone you buy in 2027 costs more, and where
the increase actually comes from — which is not where the headline says.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: "And here's the strange part. The chip isn't the expensive bit."
Paid off: at "So a double-digit chip increase is the small number on the bill
of materials", after the DRAM 58-63% and J.P. Morgan beats have earned it.
S18 return: the ending puts the two clocks side by side — Qualcomm's margins
recover in a couple of quarters, the viewer's bill lands a year later.

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: Snapdragon prices rise double digits, effective today.
WHY: broad input-cost inflation, and inside it, a memory shortage created by
AI data-centre demand — Qualcomm's rise is a pass-through, not a margin grab.
SO WHAT: the chip announced in Maui on September 22 is the first one sold at
the new price, and it goes into 2027 flagships, watches and Arm laptops.

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- Qualcomm Q3 FY26 financials (EPS $2.21, revenue $9.95B) — a stock story, not
  a phone story.
- The Galaxy S27 Ultra price-leak figures ($1,399 / $1,499, KRW 150,000) and
  the "$300 Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 Pro" chip cost — all leak-tier. Naming a
  number that specific would be spoken harder than its evidence (S20).
- Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 specs (2nm, 5GHz) — rumour, and irrelevant to the
  price story.
- J.P. Morgan's "DRAM up 400% since the start of 2024" and its CPI/PCE
  inflation estimate — true and sourced, but the TrendForce quarter-on-quarter
  number lands harder in one sentence and two macro stats in a row is a list.
- NAND +70-75% QoQ — same beat as DRAM, no new understanding.
- Reuters could not independently verify Bloomberg's letter; the script leans
  on Amon's on-the-record confirmation instead, so the letter never has to
  carry a claim.

## SOURCES

CNBC (Amon interview + Qualcomm release quote), Motley Fool transcript of the
Qualcomm Q3 FY2026 earnings call, Qualcomm.com (Snapdragon Summit dates),
TrendForce press centre (DRAM contract prices), J.P. Morgan Global Research
(AI memory shortage), 9to5Google and Android Authority (coverage + Bloomberg's
report of the customer letter). Six independent domains; four are primary.
