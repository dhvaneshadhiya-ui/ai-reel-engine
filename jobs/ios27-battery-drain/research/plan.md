# Research plan — ios27-battery-drain (news / explainer)

**Question:** After updating to iOS 27, iPhones drain faster. Is that normal
post-update behaviour, a real bug, or both — how long does it last, what
actually causes it, and what should a viewer do (and NOT do) about it?

Written 2026-09-19, before any search. Required news subtopics, one agent each.

1. **PRIMARY SOURCE** — Apple's own words: support pages on battery after an
   update, iOS 27 release notes / 27.x fix notes, anything Apple says about
   indexing, Photos analysis, Spotlight re-index after an update. Dates, exact
   wording, version numbers.
2. **INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION + SCALE** — 9to5Mac (the ref), plus other outlets
   and user reports (Apple Support Communities, MacRumors forums, Reddit) that
   did not copy each other. Record VIA. How widespread, which devices, when it
   started.
3. **CONTEXT, NUMBERS, WHAT TO DO** — what the settling period actually is
   (days), what is measurable in Settings > Battery, the concrete steps a
   viewer can take, which "fixes" are myths, and what would CONTRADICT the
   "don't panic" framing (a genuine iOS 27 battery bug, a fix in a point
   release, a device that never settles).
4. **VISUALS** — official Apple images/pages, the 9to5Mac article page, the
   Instagram reel this was referenced from, and which screens we can capture
   ourselves on an iPhone (Settings > Battery, Battery Health, Background App
   Refresh). URLs + what each shows + which beat it could prove.
