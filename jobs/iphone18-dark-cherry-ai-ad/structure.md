# Structure — iphone18-dark-cherry-ai-ad

Written BEFORE the first sentence. Framework:
`styles/shortform-script-framework.md` (S17 shapes; S25 standard).

## STORY ENGINE (framework §4A)

A viewer who's indifferent to the rumored "Dark Cherry" iPhone 18 Pro
color discovers that a fully AI-generated, clearly-fake ad for it already
changed a real tech editor's mind -- which matters because in three days
Apple has to make the ACTUAL phone live up to a color that a machine
invented first, and because it means AI content can now do a product's
own marketing job before the product exists to be marketed.

## SHAPE (S17)

Discovery.

Chosen because the spine isn't "here are iPhone 18 Pro colors" (that's
the `iphone18-colors` reel, already shipped, and repeating its Pantone/
plum reveal here would be the exact treatment-repeat RULES.md bans) --
it's "a fake thing did a real thing's job." The discovery is: this ad
isn't real, isn't Apple's, and worked anyway. Everything about Dark
Cherry itself (what it's rumored to look like, who leaked it) is
supporting evidence for THAT discovery, not the headline.

## PROMISE (S2)

You'll see the ad that did this, understand why it's legal/fine (it's
labeled), and know exactly what's confirmed vs. guessed about the real
color before Tuesday.

## OPEN LOOP (S10)

Planted: the hook states a phone and a color that both don't exist yet,
and that it already worked on someone whose job is to be skeptical of
this stuff.
Paid off: the ending returns to the real event three days out -- the
actual color now has to beat the fake one, which is the twist the whole
reel has been building to (S18: the loop closes on the real test, not on
"and that's Dark Cherry").

## WHAT -> WHY -> SO WHAT (S7)

WHAT: An AI-generated, disclosed-as-fake iPhone ad in the rumored Dark
Cherry finish changed a 9to5Mac editor's opinion on the color.
WHY: AI video is now good enough to simulate not just the PRODUCT but
the entire marketing moment around it -- convincingly enough that
disclosure ("this is fake") didn't blunt the effect.
SO WHAT: The bar for Tuesday's real unveiling was just set by something
that isn't real. If the actual Dark Cherry phone underwhelms next to the
AI version, that's now a comparison people will actually make.

## WHAT WAS CUT (S11, S21)

- The Silver-in/out lineup dispute between leakers (Sonny Dickson vs.
  Fixed Focus Digital) -- interesting but a second open loop the reel
  doesn't have room to close; it's advice-tier noise next to the AI-ad
  story, not the point.
- Apple's own past AI-ad controversies (Bella Ramsey Siri ad lawsuit,
  the "lazy" iPad ad apology) -- real, well-sourced, but a DIFFERENT
  story (Apple's AI marketing problems) that would hijack this reel's
  loop. Cut for momentum, not because it's false.
- New CEO John Ternus running his first product launch -- true and
  dated to this event, but orthogonal to the color/AI-ad story; would
  need its own setup to land.
- Exact Pantone codes for every other color (Light Blue 2121, Dark Gray
  426C, Silver 427C) -- density the `iphone18-colors` reel already
  spent; this reel only needs Dark Cherry's own code as evidence the
  color is a specific, tracked thing and not just marketing copy.
