# Script — chatgpt-stickers

Format: `ai-tools` · Style: `utility` · Target ~55s · Register: neutral

## NARRATION

Those aren't downloaded stickers. That's my own face, and ChatGPT built the whole pack.

No cutting anything out, no third-party app. It's already in the app on your phone.

Open the plus menu, tap Create image, then Stickers.

You don't type a prompt. You fill in a sentence: a style, a photo, the reactions you want.

Nineteen styles. 3D, Anime, Holographic, Chrome, Pixel Art, down to Traditional Tattoo.

Pick one, and it cuts the background out for you. That checkerboard is a real transparent PNG.

And it doesn't make one. Nine stickers, same face, nine different reactions.

Then Add to chat apps, and choose WhatsApp.

You need at least three for that button to work. That's WhatsApp's rule, not ChatGPT's.

Name the pack, and it's sitting in your keyboard.

Comment STICKERS and I'll send you the exact setup I used for these.
