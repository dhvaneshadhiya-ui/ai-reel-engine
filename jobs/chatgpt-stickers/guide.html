The complete setup

ChatGPT Sticker Packs

One photo into nine WhatsApp stickers, without a cutout app or a single paid tool. Every step below was read off a real session, not a press release.

What you need

Three things, and you probably have all of them.

The ChatGPT app on your phone — not the website, this one is mobile only. A free account, no Plus required. And one photo: a clear, well-lit face shot works best, and it can be you, a friend, or a pet.

The seven steps

Order matters here, so they are numbered.

  1. Open ChatGPT and tap the + next to the message box.

  2. Tap Plugins, then Create image.

  3. On the Create an image panel, tap the Stickers tile.

  4. You get a fill-in-the-blanks sentence, not a prompt box:

    Create a style sticker pack based on photo, remixing with emoji.

    Tap each highlighted part in turn.

  5. Tap the style slot to open Choose a style, and scroll. There are nineteen of them — the full list is below.

  6. Tap the photo slot and pick your image. Tap the emoji slot and pick a few that match the moods you want.

  7. Tap Generate. You get nine stickers in a 3×3 grid on a transparent background.

Then: Add to chat apps → WhatsApp → name your pack → Add to WhatsApp. It lands in your sticker keyboard.

The emoji are doing more work than they look like they are. They are not decoration — each one steers a different reaction in the finished pack. 🤟 produced the confident thumbs-up poses, 💥 produced the WOW and HA HA comic bursts, 🌹 produced the offering-a-rose sticker.

So pick emoji for the range of moods you want, not for how they look.

The prompt it generates

When you tap Generate, ChatGPT expands that sentence into a full prompt and sends it. The part visible in the recording reads:

…expression, pose, or reaction. Separate the stickers with wide, fully transparent gaps. No background, shadows, or overlapping elements.

The opening scrolled off screen before it was captured, so it is not reproduced here.

The three worth starting with.

3D and Clay hold a face best. Chibi and Kawaii push toward cartoon and lose the likeness. Meme is the one that actually produces reaction stickers. Holographic and Chrome look great on their own and read poorly at sticker size in a chat.

The rules that trip people up

The first one stops most people's first export.

  • You need at least three stickers to export to WhatsApp. This is Meta's rule for every app that exports to WhatsApp, not a ChatGPT limit. A pack of nine clears it easily. A single sticker will not export.
  • A WhatsApp pack maxes out at 30. One generation gives you nine, so this never binds unless you are combining packs.
  • Export targets are iMessage, WhatsApp and Save to photos. Those three.
  • Name the pack when it asks. That name is what shows in WhatsApp when you use or share it, so it is worth a moment.

If it goes wrong

Four things that actually happen.

The background isn't transparent.

Say “make the background fully transparent” and regenerate. The checkerboard pattern behind the stickers is how you confirm it worked.

The stickers are touching or overlapping.

That is exactly what “wide, fully transparent gaps” in the prompt prevents. Add it and regenerate.

It doesn't look like the person.

Use a sharper, better-lit, front-facing photo, and move toward 3D or Clay. The heavily stylised options are supposed to drift.

There's no Stickers tile.

Update the app. The feature rolled out on 24 August 2026.
