MUSIC BEDS — add your own (royalty-free)
----------------------------------------
The engine plays a music bed under every reel. Royalty-free beds were
removed from this public download for licensing reasons.

Drop your own instrumental, royalty-free tracks here (e.g. from Uppbeat,
Pixabay, or the YouTube Audio Library) and point config.json ->
defaults.musicBed at one. The examples reference "music/bed-184.mp3".
The engine masters the final mix to -14 LUFS, so any clean bed works.
